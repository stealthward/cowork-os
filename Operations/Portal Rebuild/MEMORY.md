## Key Decisions

- Per-team pages (Brett, 2026-09-09): each escrow unit gets its own page from ONE standardized template, generated from TEAMS config, as its daily landing ("portal within a portal"). Personalization is limited to defined slots (motto, accent from brand palette only, announcement) fed by a lead-editable content sheet; the portal reads it cached, no deploy needed. Common assets stay centrally configured and are referenced, never copied. Team page shows team-level pulse and Closing This Week from the team's Smartsheet.
- Roster and profiles instead of a team photo (Brett, 2026-09-09): team pages show individual headshots that open a person's profile (name, role, team, ext, tap-to-email, tap-to-call, editable fun fact). One shared people sheet feeds the team rosters, the profile cards, AND the Directory page, single source, no duplication; headshots and fun facts are self-service edits.

- Fee schedule is now a SINGLE FORMULA (Brett approved 2026-08-28, replacing the manual tier table): fee(P) = max( min(1650 + 5.00·P/1k, 1900 + 4.50·P/1k, 5650 + 2.50·P/1k, cap 20000), 2.00·P/1k ). Derived from the old table's structure; bases bumped +$150 so it's revenue-neutral-to-positive where volume clusters (Brett: most deals $750K-$1.9M; avg +$51/deal there, max deviation ~$150 from old quotes). Removes the tier table's 50+ non-monotonic cliffs. Schedule covers $100K-$30M (ceiling raised from $20M 2026-08-28 to future-proof for jumbo deals; the 0.200% line simply extends). Brett's call: do not calibrate the top end to the one $23M McInerney file ($42,450 booked, escrow 13654-BH); it sits ~8% below the extended 0.200% line, a normal negotiated discount. Under $100K / over $30M shows "check with management," never a guessed fee.
- Historical source semantics (for reference): the old per-team Sheets used $100K tiers, grey left column = tier; team typed the price into the tier row. $100K-$400K flat $2,000/$2,500/$3,000/$3,500; $500K-$5.9M price x tier rate; $6M-$9.9M $20,000 flat; $10M-$20M 0.200%.
- Split outputs (Brett 2026-08-28): total fee splits evenly, Seller shown first (seller takes the odd dollar). Investor toggle under the Seller card sets the seller side to flat $750 with the buyer half unchanged; total = $750 + buyer half. Seller credit added 2026-09-14: toggle also shows normal-seller-half minus $750 as a credit line.
- Wholesale (Concurrent Close) calculator (Brett, 2026-09-14): second section on ?page=calc from the flat-tier rate schedule, updated same day to the current sheet (CALCW in Portal.gs; five price tiers; acquisition wholesaler-own $1,000 flat, the wholesaler side only with the seller paying standard rate; acquisition buyer-both $2,500-$5,000; disposition leg total $1,500-$2,500, split evenly if both pay; extra: loan processing to escrow $200). Client name kept OFF the portal: the arrangement is being offered to multiple clients. Source PDFs in Brett's Downloads.
- Portal routing: `?page=resources` and `?page=calc` on the Unit Dashboards web app; no-param default still serves the DEFAULT_TEAM dashboard so existing team bookmarks are untouched (revisit at cutover).
- Team Smartsheet links in TEAMS config are API permalinks (get_sheet_summary), more authoritative than scraping the Sites buttons.
- Hub branding: navy #031356, Playfair Display headings, Open Sans body, official Canva brand kit logo and star marks embedded as data URIs (Sites-hosted and external images do not load inside the Apps Script sandbox).
- Per-team Fee Calculator buttons removed from the Sites portal 2026-08-28; the shared calculator page is the only fee source teams can reach. The per-team Sheets remain in Drive until phase 2 archival.

- The four hidden fee report pages (Opened Today/Yesterday/MTD, Closed Last Month) and any embedded Smartsheet views are NOT migrated: the Unit Dashboards and Revenue Dashboard supersede them (Brett, 2026-08-28). The Fee Reports group in the new portal points at the dashboards instead. Team Smartsheet buttons (outbound links for working in Smartsheet) are unaffected.
