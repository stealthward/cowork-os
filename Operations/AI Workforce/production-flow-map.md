## List It Live and Market Movers, one week

1. **Booking.** Cassie reviews active client listings in Zoho and sends invites by email template or text; status flips to Invitation Sent. Michelle confirms slots on the booking sheet (blue), sends Google Calendar invites, verifies the listing exists in Zoho (creates it from MLS if not), triggers the Appearance Scheduled confirmation through a Zoho workflow, turns the row green. Capacity six, eight max.
2. **Prep.** Michelle pulls agent production from CRMLS Matrix into Zoho and the booking sheet, adds social profiles and headshots, builds property decks and the final deck from the SOP doc. Alyza builds reel thumbnails from Canva brand templates and uploads to Drive, then tags Cassie and Elizabeth for review. Michelle writes LIL captions by pasting the MLS property description into the ChatGPT captions project (two-sentence and four-sentence versions), then updates the IG and FB caption sheets. Cassie QCs thumbnails, cross-checks decks against CRMLS, condenses photos to eight to twelve, approves the final deck to Mark over Elevate, and sends the guest list and a pre-show brief to the media team chat in Elevate and to the host. Cassie texts MM guests the day before with topic ideas.
3. **Show day.** Mark runs the two-set checklist and films. Cassie greets, walks guests through decks, films BTS, logs deck edits in production notes, sends them to editors over the Gmail chat, uploads BTS to Drive.
4. **Post.** Mark uploads RAW to the LIL and MM Drive trees and spot-checks segments. Editors cut reels and segments. Alyza QCs LIL reels (captions, thumbnail, B-roll match, name, phone, address, DRE number against Zoho), sends edit notes to the Gmail chat, posts reels through Zoho Social with captions from the sheets, tags agents on Facebook, adds shortened Drive and Instagram links to the Zoho listing record. For MM, Alyza QCs, downloads transcripts, generates IG and FB captions in the ChatGPT project, updates the caption sheets, confirms edits, posts to Instagram, adds links to the appearance record. Cassie QCs finished assets, uploads segments to Facebook and YouTube, adds links to Zoho, flips the listing or appearance status to Appearance Complete, which fires the Zoho workflow that emails the agent their assets. Cassie renames MM segments with titles generated in ChatGPT from the transcript.
5. **Engagement.** Cassie audits guest posts, follows up on pending Instagram collaborations with a template, likes and comments from the RTM reels account, and runs the untagged-agents follow-up with Loom links.
6. **Monthly.** Michelle duplicates the booking sheet tab and creates next month's Drive folder trees for both shows.

## What Brett rejects, and what a bad caption looks like

When Brett sends a piece back, it is usually the flow of information on the reel, the thumbnail title, or occasionally the caption. Two of those three are visual and live with the editor, not with any copy tool.

Bad captions are rare now. When they happened, the fixes were audience relevancy, too close to the video transcript, and carousel copy or thumbnail headlines. Brett catches them on RTM. Cassie or Jen catch them on FSE.

## The A/B/C sort

Elizabeth's answers to Part 4 of the interview, her reasons quoted. A means hand it off and a person checks. B means AI drafts and a person finishes. C means a person does it and AI stays out.

| Candidate | Sort | Elizabeth's reason |
|---|---|---|
| Captions (LIL property, MM reel and post, closing posts, highlight reels) | A | Mostly being done by AI anyway |
| Segment titles from transcripts | B | Due to the format of how the titles are used |
| Reel fact check before QC (name, address, phone, DRE, price against Zoho and the caption sheet) | A | Not really saving too much time, but slightly one less thing |
| Collecting published links, shortening, writing to Zoho fields | A/B | Could be done by AI, but links are usually done right after posting, so human would be sitting right there as it's happening. Depends on what input is needed from human (if any) to make it happen |
| Pre-show brief research on confirmed guests | B | It's important work for the human |
| Building the weekly Social Content Review task and subtasks | A/B | Could be done by AI, but it depends on what input is needed from human to ensure quality decision making (scheduling/content type), and some assets are needed by human to complete subtasks |
| Drafting next month's RTM and FSE content map | A/B | Could be done by AI, but it depends on what input is needed from human to ensure quality decision making (scheduling/content type) |
| Creating next month's Drive folders and booking sheet tabs | A | Drive and booking sheet tabs should be no issue, but not sure about creating Zoho records (which is included in the same task as Drive and booking sheet) |
| **Her addition:** Creating podcast Drive folders and adding to fields in Asana editing tasks | A | Easy, something Michelle can trigger and QC after |
