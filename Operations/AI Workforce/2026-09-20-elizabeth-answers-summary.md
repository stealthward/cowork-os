**The tool-to-tool work is Michelle's.** Asked which steps exist only because a person moves information between two tools, she answered Michelle's prep tasks and the podcast asset delivery tasks, on close to every project.

**What the team needs from Brett.** Social posts: post copy, post type, approved caption, in a Google Doc. Marketing materials: all copy, format, layout, additional considerations, and examples, in a detailed Google Doc or Asana task description.

**Her tools, in order.** Asana, Gmail, Elevate, Canva, Drive, Duda, Zoho. Duda is new to us and is not in the systems registry.
