# Google Product Marketing Manager, Retail Ads

_Condensed course-demo excerpt based on the role description used in Cowork Academy Lesson 5.02. Job postings change, so use this file for the lesson rather than as a current employment listing._

## Role Summary

Google's B2B2C Shopping and Retail Ads team is looking for a Product Marketing Manager to build narratives for agentic commerce and AI retail solutions. The role focuses on senior retail and commerce audiences.

## Core Responsibilities

- Develop the annual B2B retail and commerce messaging framework.
- Create sales narrative decks, strategic updates, and messaging houses.
- Validate positioning with research, data, and message testing.
- Draft enablement materials and campaign assets, including work produced with generative AI.
- Script keynotes and executive presentations.
- Coordinate with product marketing, regional teams, sales, and thought-leadership partners.

## Qualifications

- Six years of experience in product marketing, brand strategy, corporate communications, or narrative building in technology, retail, or advertising.
- Experience creating B2B narratives, sales-enablement materials, or messaging frameworks.
- Strong stakeholder alignment, communication, and project-management skills.
- Experience translating industry trends and audience needs into clear positioning.

## Signals To Notice

- B2B retail and commerce narratives
- Agentic commerce and AI retail solutions
- Sales enablement and messaging systems
- Data-backed positioning
- Senior executive audiences
- Cross-functional influence
- Keynotes and presentations
