# Duolingo Product Marketing Manager, Duolingo Math

_Condensed course-demo excerpt based on the role description used in Cowork Academy Lesson 5.02. Job postings change, so use this file for the lesson rather than as a current employment listing._

## Role Summary

Duolingo is looking for a Product Marketing Manager to lead marketing strategy and go-to-market execution for Duolingo Math. The role connects the product with learners, parents, and teachers while supporting awareness, engagement, and retention.

## Core Responsibilities

- Build product-marketing strategies for acquisition, engagement, and retention.
- Coordinate launches across product management, design, engineering, and marketing.
- Conduct consumer research and competitive analysis.
- Develop positioning, messaging, and go-to-market plans for products and features.
- Create campaigns across in-app communications, app-store optimization, social media, email, and content.
- Measure campaign performance and recommend improvements.
- Maintain consistent Duolingo Math messaging across teams.

## Qualifications

- Product-marketing experience in consumer technology, ideally with successful product launches.
- Familiarity with mobile apps and consumer digital products.
- Experience with educational products for children, mobile apps, games, or classroom tools is helpful.
- Strong storytelling, analytical, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

## Signals To Notice

- Consumer product marketing
- Education and learning products
- Acquisition, engagement, and retention
- Product launches
- Research and competitive analysis
- Mobile-app marketing
- Cross-functional execution
