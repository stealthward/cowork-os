# LinkedIn Senior Product Marketing Manager II

_Condensed course-demo excerpt based on the role description used in Cowork Academy Lesson 5.02. Job postings change, so use this file for the lesson rather than as a current employment listing._

## Role Summary

LinkedIn's Ventures Product Marketing team validates and incubates new product ideas for LinkedIn Talent Solutions. This senior role helps bring AI-driven products to market by influencing the roadmap and shaping positioning, messaging, validation, and go-to-market plans.

## Core Responsibilities

- Lead market validations, early charters, and go-to-market work for new product ideas.
- Develop go-to-market strategy for AI-driven and other innovative products.
- Help shape product-roadmap priorities and launch strategy.
- Create sales narratives, pitch strategy, presentations, and enablement content.
- Align product, sales, marketing, and enablement stakeholders.
- Improve the quality, speed, and scalability of the product-launch program, including the use of AI.

## Qualifications

- Ten years of experience in product marketing, go-to-market, or sales enablement.
- Experience taking new business opportunities from zero to one.
- Experience coordinating multi-product launches in enterprise technology.
- Strong narrative, visual-communication, stakeholder-management, and prioritization skills.
- HR technology experience is preferred.

## Signals To Notice

- Senior product-marketing scope
- AI-driven products
- Zero-to-one validation
- Product-roadmap influence
- Enterprise go-to-market
- Sales narratives and enablement
- Cross-functional leadership
