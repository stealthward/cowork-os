# Health Screening Reference

_Your health screening worksheet: a risk-profile template and a verification checklist for deciding which screenings apply to you. It does not give fixed medical recommendations; it prompts you to check each screening against current published guidelines and your clinician's advice at the time you use it. Read when preparing annual checkup recommendations or cross-checking screening choices. Does not contain annual checkup procedure steps (see `Health/Health Resources/Annual Health Checkup Workflow SOP.md`) or specialist visit processing steps (see `Health/Health Resources/Specialist Visit Processing SOP.md`)._

## Screenings Often Added Without Clear Basis (verify, do not assume)

_Checkup packages sometimes add these. They are commonly questioned for asymptomatic adults, but this is not blanket medical advice: treat each as an example to verify against current guidelines and your clinician before accepting or declining. Run a web search for the relevant body (USPSTF, ACS, NCI) for your situation._

| Example test | Why it is often questioned | Guideline / source to check |
|---|---|---|
| Tumor markers (e.g., AFP, CEA, PSA, CA19-9) | Often not recommended for asymptomatic screening | Check current USPSTF / NCI guidance |
| Low-dose CT (lung) | Typically reserved for specific higher-risk groups | Check current USPSTF guidance |
| Stool occult blood | Colorectal screening start age is guideline-driven | Check current USPSTF / ACS guidance |
| Thyroid ultrasound | Screening asymptomatic adults is often advised against | Check current USPSTF guidance |
