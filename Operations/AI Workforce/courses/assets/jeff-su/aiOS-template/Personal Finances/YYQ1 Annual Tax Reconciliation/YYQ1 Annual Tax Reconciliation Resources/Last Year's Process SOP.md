# Last Year's Process SOP

_Personal-finance SOP for reconciling a year of personal income and deductible expenses into a filing-ready summary. Read when starting the annual reconciliation, or when you ask "what did we do last year." Does not contain the act of filing or paying tax (that stays on your plate) and does not contain business bookkeeping._

_See also: `Personal Finances/CLAUDE.md` (parent workstation rules)._

---

## Purpose

Turn a year of scattered income and expense documents into one accurate, sourced, filing-ready summary, using a recorded process so each year's reconciliation repeats the last instead of starting from scratch.

---

## Trigger

The year-end tax documents have arrived (year-end pay statement, year-end tax forms from any payer or institution), or you ask to start the annual reconciliation, or you ask what last year's process was.

---

## Scope

**Includes:**
- Gathering and verifying income documents for the tax year.
- Tallying deductible expenses against the allowed categories.
- Producing a single reconciliation summary, every line sourced to a document.

**Does not include:**
- Filing the return, paying tax, or submitting anything to a tax authority. That stays on your plate (see `Personal Finances/CLAUDE.md`).
- Business income or expenses.

---

## Inputs

- Year-end pay statement(s) showing total income and tax withheld.
- Year-end tax forms from any payer, bank, or institution.
- Deductible-expense receipts and records for the year (sorted by category).
- Last year's reconciliation summary, for category continuity.

---

## Preconditions

- All income documents for the full tax year are in hand (no missing months or payers).
- The deduction categories and form names have been confirmed unchanged since last year, or any change has been noted.
- Source documents are saved in the project's statements/records folder, not just open in an inbox.

---

## Steps

1. **Collect.** Gather every income document and deductible record for the tax year into one place. Confirm the date range covers the full year with no gaps.
2. **Tally income.** From each income document, record gross income and tax already withheld. Carry exact figures to the cent. Note the source document beside each number.
3. **Tally deductions.** Group deductible expenses by the allowed categories (matching last year's categories). Sum each category. Set aside anything you are unsure qualifies and flag it rather than including it silently.
4. **Build the summary.** Lay out total income, total withholding, total deductions by category, and the resulting taxable figure. Every line references its source document.
5. **Cross-check against last year.** Compare category totals and structure to last year's summary. Investigate any category that swung sharply or appeared/disappeared, since that often signals a missing or double-counted document.
6. **Flag the uncertain.** List every flagged item (uncertain deduction, missing document, figure that did not tie out) in a short "needs your call" section at the end of the summary.

---

## Verification

- Income in the summary equals the sum of all income documents, to the cent.
- Total withholding in the summary equals the sum of withholding shown across all documents.
- Every line in the summary names a source document; no unsourced numbers remain.
- The flagged-items list is either empty or explicitly resolved with your input.

---

## Outputs

- One filing-ready reconciliation summary for the tax year, saved in this project's Resources folder.
- An archived set of source documents kept as the primary record.
- An updated flagged-items list, resolved or handed to you for a decision.

---

## Exceptions

- **Missing a document.** If an income or deduction document is missing, do not estimate. Record the gap in the flagged-items list and pause that line until the document arrives.
- **A category or form changed.** If a deduction category or tax form differs from last year, follow the new definition, update this SOP's Steps, and record the change in the Changelog.
- **Figures will not tie out.** If income or withholding does not reconcile to the documents, stop and surface the discrepancy rather than forcing a balance.
