# YYQ1 Annual Tax Reconciliation

_Parent workstation: Personal Finances_

## Goal

Reconcile last year's personal income and deductible expenses into a clean, filing-ready summary, and leave behind a documented process so next year's reconciliation is a repeat, not a rediscovery.

## Project-Specific Workflow

Follow the SOP first. Read `YYQ1 Annual Tax Reconciliation Resources/Last Year's Process SOP.md` at the start of every session: it is the recorded step-by-step from the last successful reconciliation and is the answer to "what did we do last year." Work the SOP top to bottom; only deviate when a source document or rule has actually changed, and record any change in the SOP's Changelog.

## Project-Specific Rules

- **Preserve exact figures.** Income amounts, withholding, and deductible totals are load-bearing. Carry them to the cent from source documents; round only in plain-language summaries, never in the reconciliation itself.
- **Source every number.** Each line in the summary points back to a specific document (a pay statement, a year-end tax form, a receipt). An unsourced number is a flag, not a fact.
- **No filing or submission action.** This project prepares the reconciliation. Actually filing, paying, or submitting anything stays on your plate; surface what is ready and wait.
