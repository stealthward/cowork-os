# Workspace Structure Tree

_What the finished workspace looks like. Read this to check workspace-level structure or see where a folder belongs. Does not contain routing policy (see root `CLAUDE.md`) or creation templates (see the `Workspace Templates` files)._

---

## Structure

Workstation-level layout. Active projects live inside their workstation and are tracked in root `MEMORY.md`, not here.

```
[your workspace]/
├── CLAUDE.md (routing map + conventions)
├── MEMORY.md (cross-cutting context)
├── ARCHIVE.md (changelog + archived memory)
├── 00_Reference/ (cross-cutting reference shelf)
├── 97_Skills/ (your skill source files, one per subfolder)
├── 98_Temp/ (temporary files, cleaned up by your session audit)
├── 99_Root Archive/ (retired root-level material only; completed projects archive inside their workstation)
├── Comms HQ/
│   └── Comms HQ Resources/
├── LinkedIn HQ/
│   └── LinkedIn HQ Resources/
├── Newsletter HQ/
│   └── Newsletter HQ Resources/
├── Dark Console Studio/
│   └── Dark Console Studio Resources/
├── Personal Finances/
│   └── Personal Finances Resources/
├── Health/
│   └── Health Resources/
├── Housing/
│   └── Housing Resources/
├── Travel/
│   └── Travel Resources/
├── Friends & Family/
│   └── Friends & Family Resources/
└── Clarity Partner/
    └── Clarity Partner Resources/
```

These are the pre-built workstations. Keep the ones that fit your work, rename or remove the rest, and add new ones as you need them (see `00_Reference/Workspace Templates - Workstations.md`).
