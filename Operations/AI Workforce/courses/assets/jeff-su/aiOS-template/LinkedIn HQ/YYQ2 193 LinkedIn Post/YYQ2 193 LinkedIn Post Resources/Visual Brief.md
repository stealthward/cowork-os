## Piece

A six-panel carousel that teaches a simple three-step framework. Pairs with LinkedIn post #193 if the post needs visual support.

---

## Specs

- **Format:** 4x5 portrait (1080 x 1350), static panels, one image per panel.
- **Panels:** 6 (hook, three framework steps, payoff, CTA).
- **Style:** Dark Console house style. Dark background, monospace label font for kickers and labels, clean sans for body, one restrained accent color used only on the active step and the CTA. No clutter; one idea per panel.
- **Through-line:** a small step indicator (01 / 02 / 03) appears on the three step panels so the sequence reads at a glance.

---

## Panel-by-Panel

### Panel 1 - Hook

- **Kicker (mono):** THE 3-STEP FRAMEWORK
- **Headline:** Most people skip the step that actually makes it work.
- **Note for studio:** this is the visual hook a scroller sees in the feed. Make the headline the loudest element. No step indicator on this panel.

### Panel 2 - Step 01

- **Step indicator:** 01
- **Label (mono):** CAPTURE
- **Body:** Get everything out of your head and into one place first. Sorting comes later.

### Panel 3 - Step 02

- **Step indicator:** 02
- **Label (mono):** CLARIFY
- **Body:** Turn each raw item into one clear next action. Vague stays stuck.

### Panel 4 - Step 03

- **Step indicator:** 03
- **Label (mono):** COMMIT
- **Body:** Put the action where you will actually see it. Out of sight is out of done.

### Panel 5 - Payoff

- **Kicker (mono):** WHY IT WORKS
- **Headline:** Capture is easy. Most people quit before Commit, so nothing ships.
- **Note for studio:** accent color carries the word "Commit." This is the turn.

### Panel 6 - CTA

- **Kicker (mono):** YOUR MOVE
- **Headline:** Save this and run all three on your next stuck task.
- **CTA line:** Follow for more frameworks like this.
- **Note for studio:** accent color on the CTA. Keep it clean; nothing below the CTA line.
