# YYQ2 Apartment Search in Central

_Parent workstation: Housing_

## Goal

Pick and secure a rental apartment in Central on Hong Kong Island that fits your budget, commute, and must-haves, with every candidate scored against the same criteria so the choice is defensible.

## Project-Specific Workflow

No project-specific workflow beyond the parent workstation. Follow `Housing/CLAUDE.md`. The working surface is `YYQ2 Apartment Search in Central Resources/Apartment Comparison.md`: add every candidate worth considering as a row, score it against the fixed criteria, and keep the recommendation line at the bottom current.

## Project-Specific Rules

- **Score every candidate against the same criteria.** The comparison table's columns (rent, commute, size, must-haves, lease terms) are fixed for this search. Do not add a candidate without filling every column, even if a value is "unknown," so apartments stay comparable.
- **Total rent, not headline rent.** Always capture the all-in monthly cost (base rent plus any mandatory building or parking fees), since headline rent hides real differences between buildings.
- **No application or deposit without confirmation.** Submitting an application, paying a holding deposit, or signing anything is an irreversible financial action; surface it and wait for your go-ahead.
