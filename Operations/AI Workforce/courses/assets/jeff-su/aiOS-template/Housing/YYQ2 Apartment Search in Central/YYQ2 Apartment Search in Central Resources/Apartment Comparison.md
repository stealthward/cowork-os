# Apartment Comparison

---

## Criteria (fixed for this search)

- **Neighborhood:** Central or close enough on Hong Kong Island that it still works as a Central search.
- **All-in rent:** base rent plus mandatory building and management fees. Lower is better, must stay under the cap.
- **Commute:** door to workplace, one way. Under 30 minutes required.
- **Size:** bedrooms and rough floor area.
- **Must-haves:** in-unit laundry (strong preference), natural light, no ground floor.
- **Lease terms:** deposit, lease length, pet and sublet policy.

Each apartment is rated Good / OK / Poor on the soft criteria and given a 1-to-5 overall fit at the end.
