# CEO review window — 48 hours minimum, no exceptions

Marcus (CEO, Brightline Labs) reads **every customer-facing email** himself before any
major launch goes out. Not a skim — a real read, with edits. He will not delegate this,
and he will not be rushed. Any launch timeline that doesn't reserve at least **48 business
hours** for his review is fiction.

**Why:** On the May 2026 "Compass" launch, the email sequence was finalized the night
before the planned send. Marcus got the drafts at 7pm, came back two days later with
edits to 4 of the 6 emails (tone on email 2, a pricing claim on email 5), and the launch
**slipped 4 days** — past the press embargo window we'd coordinated with two trade
publications. Jordan had to personally call both editors. This cannot happen again.

**How to apply:**
1. When building any launch timeline, work **backwards from the send date**: send date
minus 2 business days = "final drafts to Marcus" deadline. Treat that deadline as the
real one; the send date is just the output.
2. Send Marcus **complete, final-formatted drafts** — not outlines, not "90% there."
Partial drafts reset his clock; he re-reviews from scratch when copy changes.
3. Batch the whole sequence into **one review package** (single doc, emails in send
order, subject lines included). He hates piecemeal review threads.
4. If the timeline is already compressed and the 48-hour window doesn't fit, **flag it to
Jordan immediately** — don't silently compress the window. Jordan would rather move
the send date up front than discover the slip later.
5. This applies to *customer-facing email only*. Internal comms, the newsletter
([[project-newsletter-revamp]]), and social do NOT go through Marcus.

**Known edge cases:**
- Marcus travels a lot in September. For the Atlas launch ([[project-q3-launch]],
send date Sept 15), confirm his availability the week of Sept 8 *before* locking the
calendar — 48 hours only counts when he's actually reachable.
- Anything mentioning the Acme Corp partnership needs **legal sign-off from Priya
BEFORE it goes to Marcus** ([[feedback-legal-acme]]). Sequencing matters: legal first,
then CEO. Reversing it once cost a full extra review cycle.
- Dana (CMO) can pre-screen drafts to reduce Marcus's edit volume, but her OK does
**not** substitute for his. Both happened in parallel once; Marcus still made changes.
