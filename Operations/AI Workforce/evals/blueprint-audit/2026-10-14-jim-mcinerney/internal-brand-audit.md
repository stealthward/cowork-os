**Internal. Brett only.** Blind eval run 1 for blueprint-audit. Captured 2026-10-14. Sources: gather-pack.md (GP), the datasets in social-pull/, and the AI answers in ai-search/. Every finding cites its source. Post IDs refer to instagram.com/p/<ID>/, facebook.com/reel/<ID>/, and youtube.com/watch?v=<ID> (or /shorts/<ID>). ## 1. Executive Summary

Jim McInerney (DRE 01334502, eXp Realty of Southern California per the DRE record, GP 2.2) has the raw material of a strong La Jolla luxury brand. He is a 50-year La Jolla resident, and his site claims a 25-year career with "over $500 million in career sales" (jimmcinerney.com/jim-mcinerney/, GP 2.1). He specializes in remodel, new construction, and coastal development. He also owns a local media property (Discover La Jolla podcast and magazine) and produces the La Jolla Sky Show (Times of San Diego, 2026-06-26, GP 2.2). Production is heavy: 50 Instagram reels in 13 weeks, 38 Facebook Page posts in 90 days, and roughly weekly podcast episodes (social-pull/).

The footprint does not tell that story. The homepage leads with a generic superlative and template filler. Six awards carry Latin placeholder text, the "Years Of Experience" counter reads "under construction", and the strongest proof sits on a subpage. Across his profiles he is described with three career-volume figures ($500M, $365M, 415M), four brokerages (eXp, Compass, Pacific Sotheby's, Prudential), two agent license numbers, and five brand names (GP 6, items 1, 2, 6, 13). Search finds him on his name and nowhere else. He does not appear on any of six category queries for La Jolla or San Diego luxury (GP 3, queries 10 to 15), and neither Gemini nor claude.ai names him when asked for a recommendation (ai-search/gemini-1, gemini-2, claude-1). Even when asked about him by name, claude.ai once found only a 2021 logo contest page and called his footprint "thin" (claude-5).

The content machine is real but tilted toward listings and builds on his own channels: about 46 percent audience-building on Instagram and about 24 percent on the Facebook Page, against a 75 percent target. The community content he does make lives mostly under a separate brand (@discover.lajolla, the Discover La Jolla YouTube channel), so its goodwill does not attach to "Jim McInerney, La Jolla luxury agent." His warmest audience is his personal Facebook profile (median 41 likes since 2024 vs 2 on the Page), which has carried no real estate since December 2022.

Bottom line: identity and proof are fragmented, the website undercuts a credible operator, and the authority anchor (La Jolla native who builds and redevelops coastal luxury) is visible in posts but not owned anywhere a stranger or an AI tool would summarize it.

## 3. Website and Messaging Observations (website-standard A to G)

**A. Hero clarity: weak.** The hero reads "THE BEST REAL ESTATE TRANSACTION POSSIBLE" / "We specialize in San Diego's most distinctive real estate" (jimmcinerney.com, GP 2.1). It names no La Jolla, no client problem, and no outcome, and the headline is a generic superlative. The mission line "To be the most trusted name in real estate globally" overreaches for a La Jolla team. The YouTube channel repeats the same slogan (GP 2.5).

**B. Story structure: agent as hero, plus template residue.** The copy speaks in "We pride ourselves..." and "our brand... is unique and invaluable" (home). Six value headings ("High Quality Products", "Green Environment", "Comprehensive Amenities", "Professional Services", "Humanitarian Community", "Absolute Security") carry Latin placeholder text (home, GP 2.1). They read as a developer-theme default, not Jim's positioning. The homepage blog block shows three 2023 posts by "blackxmarketing" on topics unrelated to the market (GP 2.1, 6 item 15).

**C. Authority: present but buried, and partly unsafe.** The best proof ($500M, 25 years, 50-year resident, records) sits only on /jim-mcinerney/ and /about-us/, not on the home page. The About page awards block lists "2007 ET Now Real Estate Awards: Most Promising Delivery", "2014 Golden Globe Tiger Awards: Property of the Year", and four others, each with placeholder text (GP 2.1). These read as theme defaults and do not match the awards Jim claims on LinkedIn (La Jolla Light 2005, Circle of Excellence 2021, GP 2.3). The page states "Years Of Experience: under construction". The Featured Sold page claims "We Are La Jolla's #1 Real Estate Sales Team" with no source, while his own LinkedIn post cites "#54 spot in sales volume" in the county (GP 6 item 11). The site shows three review quotes with no dates, counts, or link to the 73 Google reviews.

**D. Process: generic.** The Buyers page's "As Your Agent" six steps (Details, Features, Safeguards, Partner, The Offer, Skill) are generic, and it promises "pay below list price" and "the lowest price possible" (jimmcinerney.com/buyers/). The Sellers page lists marketing deliverables (virtual tours, aerial, print), not how Jim prices, prepares, or negotiates (jimmcinerney.com/sellers/). The "Sell Your Home" page holds only a heading (jimmcinerney.com/sellers-2/).

**E. Differentiation: the real edge is off-page.** His actual edges are development and Coastal Commission fluency, native knowledge, and media ownership. They appear only in the About and bio long copy (/about-us/, /jim-mcinerney/). The home and seller pages lead with marketing deliverables any luxury team claims.

**F. Conversion: friction and trust breaks.**
- The displayed email Team@JimMcInerney.com links to relandgroup@mail.com.
- Footer Terms and Privacy links point to "#".
- The contact block shows "Marketing4McIneryney.com" (misspelled, no @) on some pages and a Gmail address on others.
- Jim's phone is shown as (858) 480-9945 on some pages and (858) 245-5542 on others.
- "Explore Southern California Communities We Serve" lists no communities.
- Featured Sold Properties shows no properties.
- Copy has typos: "Distintive", "helicoptor".
(All GP 2.1, GP 6 items 7 and 8.)

**G. Search and AI readiness: thin.** Service plus geography is explicit only in meta descriptions and subpage copy. There are no neighborhood or service-area pages, despite portals listing Del Mar, Carlsbad, Encinitas, Solana Beach, Rancho Santa Fe, and Carmel Valley (GP 2.2). Gemini's richer answers leaned on the site's bio page and YouTube, not the homepage (ai-search/gemini-5). This is a clarity finding, not technical SEO.
