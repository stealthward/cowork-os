# Discovery Meeting Agenda: Jim McInerney

_Internal, Brett only. Conversation prompts, not a script. Built from the Internal Brand Audit and the RTM Digital Brand Analysis, 2026-10-15._

## Business model

- Walk me through the last twelve months. How much was listings, buyers, land, and your own development projects?
- Where does the Nautilus build fit: your own investment, a client project, or both?
- Who's on the team today, and who does a client actually work with day to day?

## What makes you different

- When you win a listing against another La Jolla agent, what tips it your way?
- How often does your construction and entitlement experience come up with clients, and what does it change for them?

## Ideal client

- Picture the client you'd clone tomorrow. Who are they, what are they buying or selling, and at what price?
- Which clients or deals do you want less of?

## Goals, 12 to 24 months

- What does a great 2027 look like in deals, volume, or the kind of work you're doing?
- Is there a reputation you want that you don't have yet?

## Other media and channels

- What's Discover La Jolla for, in your mind: community work, business development, or both?
- Who runs your social accounts and writes the captions?
- Is LinkedIn worth anything to you, or would you rather let it go?

## Lead sources and referrals

- Where did your last ten clients come from?
- Who refers you most: past clients, other agents, builders, architects, podcast guests?
- How do you stay in touch with past clients today?

## Desired market perception

- After one Google search, what should a stranger in La Jolla say you're known for?
- Which agent's reputation in La Jolla do you respect most, and why?

## Visibility issues to clear up

- Which numbers are right: $365M, $415M, or $500M career volume? 18, 25, or 28 years?
- The "#1 team" claim and the awards on your About page: what's the source?
- Your Realtor.com bio still says Pacific Sotheby's and names Verlin, Cade, Deborah, and Catherine. Are any of them still with you?
- Which phone number and license number should appear everywhere?
- Do you know about the Maryland James McInerney showing up next to your name?

## Unique to Jim

- Your personal Facebook gets more engagement than every business account combined. How do you feel about real estate showing up there?
- Your band and Birdstock posts outperform your listings. Is that a part of your brand you're comfortable leaning into?
