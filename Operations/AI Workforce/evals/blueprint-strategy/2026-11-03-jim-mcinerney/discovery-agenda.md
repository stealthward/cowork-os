# Discovery Meeting Agenda: Jim McInerney

_Internal, for Brett. Short prompts to keep the conversation moving. No answers are assumed._

## Open (5 min)

- What did you think when you read the Analysis? Anything that surprised you or felt off?

## Business model

- Walk me through how the business runs today. Who's on the team, and what does each person own?
- When a client hires you, how much of the work is you personally, and how much is the team?
- How do the three pieces fit together for you: selling, your development projects, and Discover La Jolla? Which one pays the bills, and which one do you love most?

## Differentiators

- When you win a listing against another top La Jolla agent, why do you win?
- What do you know about zoning, the Coastal Commission, and entitlements, and where has that saved a client money?
- "Dirt to dream" came straight from you in the Fairway Road reel. Is that how you want to be known?

## Ideal client

- Describe the last two clients you'd clone. What were they buying or selling, and where?
- Sellers, buyers, developers, or landowners: who do you want more of over the next two years?
- Which La Jolla neighborhoods matter most to you?

## Goals, 12 to 24 months

- What does a great 2027 look like in sales, projects, and Discover La Jolla?
- Is there a number, a record, or a type of property you want to be known for?

## Lead sources and referrals

- Roughly where did your last 10 closings come from: past clients, referrals, other agents, developers, Discover La Jolla, portals?
- Who sends you the most business today, and do they know everything you do?
- How do you stay in touch with past clients now?

## Other media and channels

- What do you enjoy making? What feels like a chore?
- What should the magazine and podcast do for the real estate business, if anything?
- Your personal Facebook gets far more response than the business page. How do you feel about mixing the two?
- LinkedIn has been quiet since last November. Intentional, or just time?

## Desired market perception

- If a La Jolla homeowner described you to a friend in one sentence, what would you want them to say?
- Which agents do you see as your real competition? What do they get credit for that you don't?

## Visibility issues to clarify

- Which career volume is current: $500 million, $415 million, or $365 million? And which years figure?
- Which side did you represent on 1920 Spindrift?
- Rankings and awards: which are current and verifiable (the 2021 Circle of Excellence, #54 for 2024, "La Jolla's #1 team")?
- License: several recent captions show DRE 01334648. Do you know where that number came from?
- Which phone and office address should be on every profile?
- Who controls the website, Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, and Google Business Profile logins? Birdeye shows as unclaimed: do you want it claimed?
- The website still has template text and template awards. Who built it, and who maintains it?

## Specific to Jim

- The Realtor.com bio still names Pacific Sotheby's and a past roster. Any history there we should handle carefully?
- How do you want Real Talk Media's work on Discover La Jolla to relate to your personal brand going forward?
- The Real Talk Media posts that show up on your grid talk about you in the third person, because we made them for our own audience. How would you feel about dropping the collaboration and posting that kind of content yourself, in your own voice?
- Is there anything about the name (the James McInerneys in other states) that's caused confusion before?

## Close

- Confirm the next step and timing for the Blueprint.
