### Zillow

I help homeowners in La Jolla and along the San Diego coast sell luxury homes and lots for their full potential, and I represent luxury buyers and developers taking on redevelopment, land, and new construction. With more than 25 years in real estate and more than $365 million in career sales, I understand how zoning, local ordinances, and Coastal Commission review shape what a La Jolla property is worth. I am a La Jolla native and the publisher of Discover La Jolla magazine, so this town is my market and my home. When you hire me, you work directly with me through closing.

In La Jolla, the value of a home or a lot often comes down to its potential. A detail in zoning or coastal review can change a price or a plan. My role is to sort that out early, so you can decide with a clear picture.

I work alongside architects, builders, and contractors when a project calls for it. I specialize in taking dirt to dream, whether that means selling a home or maximizing the potential of a lot.

If you are selling, buying, or building in La Jolla, I would be glad to help.

### Realtor.com

I help homeowners across La Jolla and the San Diego coast sell luxury homes and lots, and I guide luxury buyers, plus developers taking on redevelopment, land, and new construction. I bring more than $365 million in career sales and more than 25 years of experience, along with a working knowledge of La Jolla zoning, local ordinances, Coastal Commission review, and the entitlement process. As a La Jolla native and the publisher of Discover La Jolla magazine, I stay close to the community I serve. You work directly with me throughout the entire process.

Along this coastline, a property's potential often matters as much as what it is today. A zoning detail or a coastal requirement can change a price or a plan. I help you see those questions early, so you can move forward with confidence.

When a project needs architects, builders, or contractors, I bring them in. I specialize in taking dirt to dream, from selling a home to planning a new build on a lot.

Whether you are selling, buying, or building in La Jolla, I would welcome the chance to help.

### Homes.com

I represent homeowners in La Jolla and along the San Diego coast selling luxury homes, along with luxury buyers and developers taking on redevelopment, land, and new construction. If you are selling, buying, or building in La Jolla, I would be honored to guide you.
