### Zillow

You want to buy, sell, or build in La Jolla, and you want to know where you stand before you decide. In La Jolla, what a house or lot is worth depends on what zoning, the Coastal Commission, and entitlement allow. A price is hard to trust when you cannot see what you may build, and you should know what a property can become before you commit.

I understand that, because La Jolla raised me. I am a La Jolla native, a graduate of La Jolla High School and USC, and I have been licensed since 2001. I have completed more than 30 La Jolla sales, publish Discover La Jolla magazine, and know these rules well.

Here is how we work. First, we look at what the site allows before any offer or listing. Second, I bring in architects, builders, or contractors where the plan needs them. Third, I personally lead your purchase or sale through closing.

Message me about the property you have in mind. You avoid paying for, or pricing, a plan the rules will not allow, and you move forward with a clear view of what the property can become.

### Realtor.com

Buying, selling, or redeveloping a La Jolla home starts with one question: what is this property allowed to become? Here, the answer shapes the price, because zoning, Coastal Commission rules, and entitlement decide what can be built. Without that answer, a price is hard to believe, and no one should commit to a property before seeing its possibilities.

I understand the uncertainty, because La Jolla is where I grew up. I went to La Jolla High School and USC, and I have been licensed since 2001. I have handled more than 30 La Jolla sales, publish Discover La Jolla magazine, and produce the annual La Jolla Sky Show. That local footing lets me explain the rules plainly.

Nobody buys or lists against a plan the zoning would refuse, and you finish knowing what the property is capable of.

We begin by reading the site against those rules. Then architects, builders, or contractors join when the plan calls for them. Throughout, I lead your purchase or sale myself, all the way to closing.

Send me the address you own or are considering, and we will start there.

### Homes.com

If you plan to buy, sell, or build in La Jolla, you are really asking what the rules will let you do. Zoning rules, Coastal Commission review, and the entitlement process set a property's value here, and without that answer a price is a guess. You should know where a property is headed before you sign anything.

My work starts with the land. I read the site against the local zoning, Coastal Commission, and entitlement limits, bring in architects, builders, or contractors when the design needs them, and lead the transaction personally through closing.

I know how unsettling it is to commit before the answers are in. I can speak to this as a La Jolla native who went through La Jolla High School and USC, and who has held a license since 2001. More than 30 La Jolla sales, the Discover La Jolla magazine I publish, and the annual La Jolla Sky Show I produce all come from the same place: this town.

Done this way, you will not pay for a design the zoning cannot support, and you will move ahead sure of what the land allows.

Reach out with an address, and we will walk it together.
