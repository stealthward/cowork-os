### Zillow

I am a La Jolla real estate agent, with a focus on luxury listings and buyers in La Jolla, plus redevelopment, land, and new construction across coastal San Diego. My edge is redevelopment knowledge, and my record includes more than 30 La Jolla sales. I personally lead every transaction.

I know La Jolla zoning, the Coastal Commission, and entitlement, and I work alongside architects, builders, and contractors. Before you buy or sell, I help you work through the zoning, Coastal Commission, and entitlement questions that apply to the property. Those questions are easier to settle before a purchase or a listing than after.

I have been licensed since 2001, and I am a La Jolla native with about 50 years in the community, a La Jolla High School graduate, and a USC graduate. I publish Discover La Jolla magazine, host the Discover La Jolla podcast, and produce the annual La Jolla Sky Show.

If a La Jolla property raises a zoning or entitlement question, start with a conversation with me before you list or make an offer.
