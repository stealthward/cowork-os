### Zillow

You may be looking to buy or sell a luxury home in La Jolla, or to take on redevelopment, land, or new construction. Either way, what a property is worth here depends on what zoning, the Coastal Commission, and entitlement allow. Not knowing what you can build leaves any price on shaky ground, and you deserve to see a property's future before you commit.

I know that uncertainty, and I know these rules. I have handled more than 30 La Jolla sales, work alongside architects and builders, and know the town as a native, licensed since 2001. I also publish Discover La Jolla magazine and host its podcast.

My process starts with the site: I review what the rules permit before any offer or listing, bring in the right builder or architect when the plan needs one, and lead the transaction myself through closing.

Message me about the property you have in mind and whether you are buying, selling, or building.

You avoid paying for, or pricing, a plan the rules will not allow.

You move forward with a clear view of what the property can become.

You should know where a property is headed before you sign.

La Jolla is home to me, and I know its zoning, the Coastal Commission, and the entitlement process. More than 30 La Jolla sales, the Discover La Jolla magazine I publish, and the La Jolla Sky Show I produce keep me close to the town.

I start by studying the site against those rules, bring in a contractor or architect when the project needs one, and stay personally in charge of each transaction.

You do not find out too late that a plan cannot be built.

You choose with the full picture of what the property could be.

Send me the address you are weighing, and I will begin with the rules that apply to it.
