### Zillow

I represent buyers and sellers of luxury homes in La Jolla and along the San Diego coast, with a focus on redevelopment, land, and new construction. With more than 25 years in real estate and more than $365 million in career sales, I understand how zoning, Coastal Commission review, and entitlement shape what a La Jolla property is worth. I am a La Jolla native and the publisher of Discover La Jolla magazine, so this town is my market and my home. When you hire me, you work directly with me through closing.

In La Jolla, the value of a home or a lot often comes down to what you are allowed to build. A detail in zoning or coastal review can change a price or a plan. My role is to sort that out early, so you can decide with a clear picture.

I work alongside architects, builders, and contractors when a project calls for it. I specialize in taking dirt to dream, whether that means selling a home or planning what a lot can become.

If you are buying, selling, or building in La Jolla, I would be glad to help.

### Realtor.com

I help buyers and sellers of luxury homes across La Jolla and the San Diego coast, including clients taking on redevelopment, land, and new construction. I bring more than $365 million in career sales and more than 25 years of experience, along with a working knowledge of La Jolla zoning, Coastal Commission review, and the entitlement process. As a La Jolla native and the publisher of Discover La Jolla magazine, I stay close to the community I serve. You work directly with me throughout the entire process.

Along this coastline, what a property can become often matters as much as what it is today. A zoning detail or a coastal requirement can change a price or a plan. I help you see those questions early, so you can move forward with confidence.

When a project needs architects, builders, or contractors, I bring them in. I specialize in taking dirt to dream, from selling a home to planning a new build on a lot.

Whether you are buying, selling, or building in La Jolla, I would welcome the chance to help.

### Homes.com

I represent buyers and sellers in La Jolla and along the San Diego coast who are buying or selling luxury homes or taking on redevelopment, land, and new construction. With more than 25 years in real estate and more than $365 million in career sales, I focus on what zoning, Coastal Commission review, and entitlement allow on a property, because in La Jolla that is often where its value is decided. I grew up here and publish Discover La Jolla magazine, which keeps me connected to the neighborhoods I work in. You will work directly with me from start to finish.

Buying, selling, or building here is a significant decision, and it rarely comes with simple answers. My job is to get the important questions answered early and to keep you informed at every stage.

I work alongside architects, builders, and contractors when a project calls for them. I specialize in taking dirt to dream, from a single home sale to a full redevelopment.

If you are buying, selling, or building in La Jolla, I would be honored to guide you.
