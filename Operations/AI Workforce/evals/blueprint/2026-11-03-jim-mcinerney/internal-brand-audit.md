## 1. Executive Summary

Jim McInerney is a long-tenured La Jolla agent (license 01334502, eXp Realty of Southern California since March 2024 per LinkedIn) with real, current proof. That includes a $17,340,000 buyer-side sale at 1920 Spindrift Drive (sold 08/27/2026 per Realtor.com; captioned "officially La Jolla's Highest Summer Sale (per MLS data)" on Instagram), 32 La Jolla sales on Homes.com's five-year view, a 5.0 Google rating on 73 reviews, and a genuine community media arm: Discover La Jolla magazine, podcast, and the La Jolla Sky Show. Search confirms the entity cleanly. All seven organic results for "Jim McInerney real estate" are his, and both ChatGPT and Gemini identify him correctly as a La Jolla luxury agent with eXp.

Underneath, the footprint does not agree with itself, and two problems are serious enough to cost trust on first contact:

1. **The website shows template placeholder content.** The homepage shows six feature blocks of Latin filler ("Magni dolores eos qui ratione...") and three 2023 blog posts by "blackxmarketing". The About page's awards carousel lists "Golden Globe Tiger Awards / Property of the Year" and "ET Now Real Estate Awards / Most Promising Delivery", each over "Nemo enim ipsam..." filler text. The CMO confirmed this on the live site (cmo-verification section 2). A luxury seller who scrolls will see it, and the template "awards" read as invented credentials.
2. **The public record contradicts itself on brokerage, experience, volume, phone, and license.** Realtor.com's bio still opens with "of Pacific Sotheby's International Realty" (he left in January 2020 per LinkedIn). The GBP description says "Through our partnership with Compass" and then ends "DRE#01334502 eXp Realty". Years in business read 25 (site), 28 (Zillow, Realtor.com), and 18 (Homes.com). Career volume reads "over $500 million" (site), "$365+million" (LinkedIn), and "topping 415 million" (Realtor.com). Four Instagram and five Facebook captions print a different license number (01334648).

The positioning is split across three stories with no hierarchy: luxury coastal sales, "taking dirt to dream" (remodels and new construction), and community publisher. Jim's bio page (/jim-mcinerney/) names remodels and custom new construction as his specialty, but no Google review uses the word "remodel" or "developer" (0 of 73). Zillow carries no bio at all. In the AI check, Jim is not named by either ChatGPT or Gemini when a consumer asks who to hire in La Jolla, including for his own stated specialty. ChatGPT instead names Rob and Joy Aumann, Jim's co-listing partner on 7145 Fairway Road.

On social, Instagram is active and consistent (5.21 posts per week, no gap over 14 days). By the CMO's classification of each caption, Jim's own posts are 74% professional (29 of 39), and most of the community side of the feed comes from Discover La Jolla and Real Talk Media collaborator posts. Facebook Page engagement is near zero (median 2). YouTube has 43 uploads with a median of 50 views and no upload since 2026-08-10. LinkedIn has been silent since 2025-11-15. The top three Instagram posts mix both: Birdstock (most likes and comments) and two on-site property reels, 7145 Fairway and 1616 Clemson (most plays). On Facebook, family posts on the personal profile far outdraw the business Page.

## 3. Website and Messaging Observations (website-standard.md A to G)

Pages reviewed: every main-navigation page (gather-pack section 1, rows 1 to 14), plus the CMO's live check of the homepage and /about-us/ (cmo-verification section 2).

**A. Hero clarity: weak.** The hero reads "THE BEST REAL ESTATE TRANSACTION POSSIBLE / We specialize in San Diego's most distinctive real estate." It names no market narrower than San Diego, no service, no client problem, and no outcome. The mission line, "To be the most trusted name in real estate globally", is generic and outsized for a La Jolla practice. The meta description ("marketing-led real estate services that expertly guide buyers, sellers, and investors through") is cut off mid-sentence. The words "La Jolla" do not appear in the hero.

**B. Story structure: agent as hero.** Homepage copy is about the team ("the area's most established and top-performing real estate experts", "Our brand ... is unique and invaluable to our clients"). The client's problem never appears. The /sellers/ page lists eight marketing deliverables (virtual tours, aerial video, print, signage) with no seller problem framing.

**C. Authority: claimed, not shown, and partly undermined.** The proof on the site is "over $500 million in career sales", "multiple luxury real estate sales records" (/jim-mcinerney/), and "La Jolla's #1 Real Estate Sales Team" (Featured Sold page). None is sourced on the page. The awards carousel is template content: "Golden Globe Tiger Awards", "ET Now Real Estate Awards", "BAM Awards Real Best" with Latin filler under each entry (cmo-verification section 2). The About counters show "Years Of Experience" and "Projects Completed" with "under construction" between them. The Featured Sold grid did not render in the gather's text capture (gather-pack section 2.8), so whether the strongest proof (Spindrift, $17.34M) appears on the site is unverified (Manual Review item 2). The awards Jim lists on LinkedIn (Circle of Excellence Platinum 2021, "Best Real Estate Agent in La Jolla" by La Jolla Light 2005; unverified) do not appear on the site.

**D. Process: missing.** /buyers/ has a six-item "As Your Agent" list (Details, Features, Safeguards, Partner, The Offer, Skill) with one-line generic copy ("We're by your side every step of the way"). There is no seller process. The construction and development process, the most differentiated thing he does, has no page. "Sell Your Home" (/sellers-2/) returns only the heading "Sellers".

**E. Differentiation: present in the bio, absent from the structure.** /jim-mcinerney/ and /about-us/ carry a real edge: "15 years immersed in luxury residential development", insight into "La Jolla's complex zoning requirements, Coastal Commission regulations, and entitlement process". It sits on a sub-page. The homepage says nothing about it.

**F. Conversion: diffuse.** CTAs are "learn more", "Work With Team McInerney", and "Let Us Sell Your Home". The contact block lists three emails (one a Gmail address) and three phones. The homepage footer shows Jim at (858) 245-5542, while subpage footers show (858) 480-9945. The general email displays as "Marketing4McIneryney.com" on every subpage (gather-pack section 2.1 and section 7).

**G. Search and AI readiness: mixed.** The page title repeats "Jim McInerney Real Estate Team" twice, and the hero carries no service plus geography pairing. The bio pages are specific and quotable, and the AI tools draw from them (ChatGPT's prompt 4 answer lists jimmcinerney.com as a source; Google's AI Overview on the magazine query cites /jim-mcinerney/). Placeholder text and unrelated 2023 blog titles ("space solutions and small apartment ideas") dilute what the page is about.

**Other site defects.** The nav item "Discover La Jolla" links back to the homepage. The Video Library page did not load on two fetches. The Instagram bio's YouTube link (@thejimmcinerneyrealestatet324) returns 404. The /karen-roque/ page's LinkedIn icon links to Jim's profile, not Karen's (gather-pack sections 1 and 2.4). The About page lists six team members (Jim, Karen Roque, Marshall McInerney, Jennifer Kim, Rick Saavedra, Mark Eidbo) while the nav lists three.
