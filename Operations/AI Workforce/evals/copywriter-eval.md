# Eval: copywriter

**What good looks like before a draft of Brett's own content reaches Brett.**

**Owner:** Brett Ward
**Used by:** the editor agent on every copywriter output; Brett rates the first ten pieces by hand as well (Phase 2 gate: ten pieces of planned content rated light pass or as-is; a rewrite is reconciled and does not count; no pieces are made just for the gate, the CMO asks for the rating in the thread where a planned piece is approved, Brett 2026-10-30)
**Version:** 1.2, 2026-11-03 (worker review: criterion 5 on Brett's scale, last copywriter version scored, rounds and token cost logged, one persona per footer); 1.1, 2026-10-06 (content-frameworks.md wired in)
**Pass bar:** weighted score of 4.0 or higher, no criterion below 3, zero auto-fails

## How to score

Score each criterion 1 to 5. Write the reasoning first, then the number. Score the last version the copywriter produced. When the CMO applied fixes or Brett swapped lines before rating, the gate row records the last copywriter version and who changed what after it; Brett's rating still counts for the gate (Brett, 2026-11-03). If the work is missing what you need to judge it (no source or frameworks named in the footer, no platform stated), mark "cannot assess" and send it back. Score against the bar, not against how much you like the draft.

## Criteria

**1. Facts match the source (weight 3)**
A 5: every claim, name, figure, and date appears in the named source or in brett-profile.md, and the footer says which. A 1: anything invented, estimated, or carried over from an earlier piece.

**2. Voice (weight 3)**
A 5: reads like Brett over coffee and passes every rule in voice-principles.md: complete sentences, no em dashes, no hashtags, no corporate tone, no motivational filler; one notch warmer than his on-camera default. A 1: fragments, staccato, lecturing, filler, or an em dash.

**3. Positioning and terms (weight 2)**
A 5: Brett is the operator, not the producer; tenure anchors on 2000, 2011, 2012 and is never a fixed year count; no "revenue share"; the Five Star partner structure, if mentioned, is worded as an employee earning commissions on escrow; give-first content carries no pitch. A 1: any of those broken.

**4. One avatar, not two (weight 2)**
A 5: the piece speaks to one persona from audience-personas.md, the footer names that one persona with no qualifiers (REACH is persona 2; never "any licensed agent" or "light for top producers"), and the two client profiles ($50M+ pursued by Brett versus 8+ list sides attracted by marketing) are not blended. A 1: the piece addresses "agents" generically or conflates the profiles.

**5. Would Brett post it after a light pass (weight 2)**
Scored from Brett's rating on his scale (Brett, 2026-11-03). A 5: as-is, or Brett changes a word or two. A 4: light pass: Brett keeps the structure and most lines, and edits, cuts, or swaps up to about a quarter of it. A 3: Brett cuts or rebuilds a section. A 1: Brett rewrites the piece or starts over.

**6. Fit for the destination (weight 1)**
A 5: the requested platform and format delivered as one labeled block each (post copy, post type, platform); scripts in spoken rhythm with a runtime estimate; REACH sections as prose under the outline heading. A 1: needs reformatting before use.

**7. Framework fit (weight 2)**
A 5: the footer names the content-frameworks.md section 2 map row and the frameworks used; the piece carries that row's primary structure; the hook hits at least two of the seven headline components (4.3) and the body pays it off; every "Editor checks" line under the named frameworks passes. A 3: the structure is there, but one "Editor checks" line misses or the footer's frameworks line is incomplete. A 1: no framework named, or the piece ignores its row (a script without hook, point, proof, close; a caption that does not pay off the clip's hook).

## Automatic fails

- Any fact not traceable to the source or the profile
- Any em dash or hashtag
- "Revenue share" or "rev share" in any form
- A fixed tenure count ("15 years," "since 2011" is fine)
- Anything posted, sent, or written to a live document by the agent itself
- Compensation, ownership, or Max Income content in any piece
- A later-lane framework (content-frameworks.md section 12) used on Brett's own content

## When a human steps in

Escalate before returning if a criterion scores below 3 or an auto-fail triggers. Brett's rating on the first ten overrides the editor's score, and a disagreement fixes this file.

## Keeping this eval sharp

When Brett disagrees with a score, fix this document, not that one score. Every miss Brett catches becomes a line in the next version and, when it is a voice miss, a line in voice-principles.md through the reconcile skill. Rerun the whole eval on every model release.
