Most agents post for the people who are buying or selling right now.

Everyone else who sees those posts is not in the market yet, and they are quietly deciding whether you are worth remembering when they are.

Good content has one test: it is interesting and relevant to the people who see it. A just-listed post passes that test for a handful of people. A post about the question every seller asks you in the first meeting, or the neighborhood spot your clients keep recommending, passes it for almost everyone.

I have spent fifteen years in escrow, and the standard I hold myself to is higher than anyone else could set for me: overdeliver on everything, and make that my standard. People don't listen to what you say. They watch what you do. When they see what you produce for yourself and for others, they trust that you will do the same for them.

Your content is where they watch. Would your next post interest someone with no plans to move? If it would, it is earning that trust before anyone needs you.
