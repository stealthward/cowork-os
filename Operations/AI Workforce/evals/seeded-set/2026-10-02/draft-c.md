Most agents post for the people who are buying or selling right now.

That group is a small slice of who sees your content on any given day. The rest of your audience is not in the market yet, and they are quietly deciding whether you are worth remembering when they are.

Good content has one test: it is interesting and relevant to the people who see it. A just-listed post passes that test for a handful of people. A post about the question every seller asks you in the first meeting, or the neighborhood spot your clients keep recommending, passes it for almost everyone.

I have been building businesses since 2000, and the lesson keeps repeating: people hire the person they already trust, and that trust gets built long before anyone needs you. Last year our escrow team closed 1,100 files, and almost every one came from an agent who trusted us before the file ever opened.

If you want a quick check this week, scroll back through your recent posts and ask how many would interest someone with no plans to move. If most of them only speak to active buyers and sellers, you already know where your next month of content should go.
