That group is a small slice of who sees your content on any given day. The rest of your audience is not in the market yet, and they are quietly deciding whether you are worth remembering when they are.

Good content has one test: it is interesting and relevant to the people who see it. A just-listed post passes that test for a handful of people. A post about the question every seller asks you in the first meeting, or the neighborhood spot your clients keep recommending, passes it for almost everyone.

I have been building businesses since 2000, and the lesson keeps repeating: people hire the person they already trust, and that trust gets built long before anyone needs you.

The question I keep coming back to is simple: would this post interest someone with no plans to move? When the answer is yes, the post is doing the real work, earning trust before anyone needs you.
