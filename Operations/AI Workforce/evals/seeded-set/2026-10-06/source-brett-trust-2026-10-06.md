# Brett's statement on trust (Brett, in the CMO thread, 2026-10-06, verbatim)

Trust is built by having higher standards for yourself than anybody else could possibly have for you. It's about overdelivering on everything you do and making that your standard. People don't listen to what you say. They watch what you do. When people see what you produce for yourself and for others, they tend to trust that you will do the same for them.
