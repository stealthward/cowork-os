# Elizabeth Interview Guide

_Prepared 2026-09-17. Brett sits in, Claude interviews through the Code tab with Asana and production-flow-map.md open. Budget 45 to 60 minutes. Every question below changes what gets built; skip anything the flow map already answers correctly._

## Before the session

1. Elizabeth reads production-flow-map.md (ten minutes). She marks what is wrong or out of date.
2. Brett confirms the session covers content operations only. Escrow operations and finance are separate lanes and separate conversations.
3. Have open: List It Live / Market Movers Recurring, the Discover La Jolla 10/02/26 project, the RTM Social Calendar, the Miro production process board.

## Part 1: Validate the flow (15 minutes)

1. Walk the LIL and MM week in the flow map. Where is it wrong, and where has the redo already changed it?
2. Which steps exist only because a person has to move information between two tools (Sheets to Zoho, Drive to Asana, Asana to Elevate)?
3. Where does work wait? Name the three handoffs that stall most often and why.
4. What in the Discover La Jolla redo is the new standard, and what is still being worked out? Which shows follow next and when?
5. What do the Asana automations do today, and which ones are being cleaned up or removed?

## Part 2: Approval gates (10 minutes)

6. Brett is the last approver. On which content lines does that happen through the weekly Social Content Review task, and where does it happen elsewhere (text, Elevate, in person)?
7. What gets posted without Brett seeing it (show reels, closing posts, segment uploads)? Who is the last check there?
8. When Brett rejects a piece, what usually needs fixing: facts, voice, visuals, or timing?
9. For campaigns and higher-level content Brett writes on the front end, what does the team need from him to run with it, and in what format?

## Part 3: The ChatGPT system (10 minutes)

10. Who uses the captions project, how often, and what do they edit after the output comes back?
11. What does a bad caption look like, and how is it caught today?
12. Are there instructions in the ChatGPT project beyond the three prompts in the task descriptions? Anything the team relies on that is not written down?
13. What would make the team stop using the migrated version? (Speed, extra steps, output that needs more editing.)

## Part 4: Sort the tasks (10 minutes)

For each recurring task in the LIL/MM project and the social calendars, sort into:

- A: hand it off. AI does it, a person checks the result.
- B: work alongside. AI drafts, a person finishes.
- C: keep. A person does it, AI stays out.

Start with the eight candidates at the bottom of the flow map. Ask for the ninth and tenth she would add.

## Part 5: Elizabeth as the pilot (10 minutes)

14. What does Elizabeth's own day look like, and which two or three things does the team come to her for that could be answered without her?
15. Which tools does she live in, in order? (Asana first, then?) The loop channel goes where she already looks.
16. What would make her trust an agent's output, and what would make her stop trusting it after one bad result?
17. Who on the VA team should touch agents first, and who should not yet?

## Part 6: Access and hygiene (5 minutes)

18. Shared logins in task descriptions (ChatGPT, Instagram): who owns moving them to 1Password, and by when?
19. Which Zoho workflow rules fire on status changes, and which ones must an agent never trigger by accident?
20. Where do the SOP docs live, and which ones are current?

## After the session

- Update production-flow-map.md with corrections the same day.
- Write the A/B/C sort into the flow map as a table.
- Log decisions in MEMORY.md under Key Decisions.
- The captions skill spec comes from Part 3; the loop channel design from Part 5.
