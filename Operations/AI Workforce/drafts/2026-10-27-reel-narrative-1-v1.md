# Reel narrative 1, v1: Factory Ep 1 R4, three legs of the stool

---

Every business we've built has stood on three legs, and when one goes missing, the stool tumbles over.

The clip covers the idea in about a minute. Here is what each leg looks like when we're building a team, and what happens when it's gone.

**Leg one is talent acquisition.** We look for what I call A players, people who come wired with their own personal standards. They hold themselves to a high bar before we ever say a word. I think of it as a puzzle: everyone has a different shape, but the shapes have to fit together. When we get this leg wrong, the people who care the most end up covering for the ones who don't. [BRETT: a time the talent leg was missing, and what it cost]

**Leg two is the business model.** An A player needs an opportunity big enough to reach their goals and achieve their dreams, and big enough to keep them motivated. If the ceiling is lower than what they want, the best people eventually go find a bigger one. [BRETT: a time the business model leg was missing]

**Leg three is culture.** We want an environment people choose to stay in for a long time. Strong people and a big opportunity still don't hold a team together if the place isn't one they want to be part of. [BRETT: a time the culture leg was missing]

Each leg depends on the other two. Great people need room to grow, and room to grow needs people who can fill it. A place worth staying in keeps both.

**Talent acquisition, business model, and culture are the three legs that let an organization keep growing and scaling.**

Miss one, and the rest can't hold the weight.
