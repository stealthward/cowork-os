## Transcript (verbatim, as transcribed)

Branding is just reputation amplified. Creating a brand and sticking to a brand and having a brand show up and be meaningful is a lot different from just having something pretty. I think a lot of people really struggle with actually what brand or what branding is. And it's because the definition has changed so much over time. I think where most people are still confused is they think brand as logos, colors, fonts, typically anything that's aesthetic. You hear terms like, well, this is on-brand and this is off-brand. And generally, they're talking about something that's visual. And the 21st century has moved so much more into a space where it's behavior-driven. And it's really more than anything else, brand is association. What do people associate your brand with when they hear it? If you look at Amazon, what's their brand? You think convenience. When you think a brand like Apple, you think aesthetic, beautiful technology that's easy to use. And so everybody thinks brand first as the aesthetic, but really it's more about your identity and the perception that other people have of you. And when you finally shift that focus, it's much more than what shows up on your banner on your website, as it is the experience that people have.

## Depth source: Ep 3 R14 (Brett speaking), "Brand damage"

you've got brand and then you've got brand damage. So for example, let's say you've got a professional who's highly polished and they're known to be detail-oriented, amazing communication, highly ethical. That's their brand. That's somebody that you say, for all of these reasons, I'm comfortable doing business with that person. Their reputation from you, previous clients, online reviews, impeccable. And then you see them at a youth sports game ripping the head off of a coach, yelling at their kid or their ref. So brand at its essence is the all-encompassing how you show up online, how you show up on print, how you show up in person, professionally and personally. And any time you do something that diverts that, you can be rebranded in an instant.
