Branding is just reputation amplified.
When people associate you with the right things, they get comfortable doing business with you.

I've seen someone take an extreme and polarizing political stance and be very public and combative about it. The first sign a brand doesn't match its reputation is when much of the content is about how successful the person is.

Anything that diverts from your brand can rebrand you in an instant.
