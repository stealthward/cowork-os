## Definition of good, per agent (seeds for the eval files)

**copywriter.** (1) Brett would post it after a light pass, not a rewrite. (2) No em dashes, no hashtags, no "revenue share," tenure never a fixed count. (3) Sounds like brett-profile.md and voice-principles.md, warmer than his on-camera default. (4) Every claim traceable to the source or the profile. (5) Delivered in the stated format, one block per platform.

**editor.** (1) Catches every rule miss in a seeded draft (five seeded misses, five caught). (2) Never rewrites; returns grade plus fix list. (3) Says "could not check" for anything without a source. (4) Passes a clean draft without inventing a fix. (5) Runs in under a minute on Sonnet.

**research-sidekick.** (1) Every fact carries a source and a date. (2) The brief answers the one question in the first three lines. (3) Nothing unverified is stated as fact. (4) No contact is made and no Zoho field is written. (5) Brett can act on the brief without opening the sources.
