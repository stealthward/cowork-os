Your logo is not why clients hire you.
They hire what they associate with you, and you build that every day.

Branding is reputation amplified. The definition has changed so much over time that a lot of people still struggle with it. When they hear "on-brand" or "off-brand," they think of something visual: logos, colors, fonts. Today brand is driven by behavior, and it comes down to one question: what do people associate with you when they hear your name?

**Association.** Think of Amazon and you think convenience. Think of Apple and you think beautiful technology that's easy to use. Neither answer is a color or a font. Brand is the identity and the perception other people have of you, and that matters far more than what shows up on the banner of your website. It's the experience people have.

**Damage.** Take a professional who is highly polished. They're known for being detail-oriented, communicating well, and being highly ethical. Their past clients and online reviews are impeccable, so people say, "For all of those reasons, I'm comfortable doing business with this person." Then you see them at a youth sports game, ripping the head off a coach or yelling at their kid or the ref. [BRETT: a moment you've seen where someone's behavior outside work changed how people saw their business, no names]

Brand is how you show up online, in print, and in person, professionally and personally. Anything you do that diverts from it can rebrand you in an instant.

**People.** This is why clients hire the person, not the company. They aren't hiring your logo or your brokerage. The more AI and polish we see, the more people look for relatability and human connection. My opinion, and it's an opinion and not data, is that a person's page runs circles around their company's page a thousand out of a thousand times, because relatability is all anybody wants. The people who position themselves best are authentic and show up with real human connection and real solutions. [BRETT: one line on how this changed how you show up yourself]

**Brand is association, and every moment you show up adds to it or takes from it.**
