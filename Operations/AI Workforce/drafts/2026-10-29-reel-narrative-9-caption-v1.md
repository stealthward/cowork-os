Brand is the number one objective for any agent who wants to grow. NAR says 66% of sellers hire a referred agent or one they've used before.

I've seen past clients go to another agent all the time, because agents don't have a systematic way to stay in front of them.

The agents I've seen do it consistently send a weekly newsletter with embedded video.

If you've got a strong brand, they'll knock on your door.
