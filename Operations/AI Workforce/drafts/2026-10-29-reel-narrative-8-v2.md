Branding is just reputation amplified.
Shape what people associate with you, and they get comfortable doing business with you.

Most people hear "on-brand" and think of logos, colors, and fonts. I define brand as association: what people think of when they hear your name. Amazon is convenience. Apple is beautiful technology that's easy to use.

**Damage.** Picture a professional who is highly polished. They're known for being detail-oriented, communicating well, and acting with high ethics. Their past clients and online reviews are impeccable, so people say, "For all of those reasons, I'm comfortable doing business with this person." Then you see them at a youth sports game, ripping the head off a coach or yelling at their kid or the ref. [BRETT: a moment you've seen where someone's behavior outside work changed how people saw their business, no names]

Your brand is how you show up online, in print, and in person, professionally and personally. Anything you do that diverts from it can rebrand you in an instant.

**People.** Clients hire people. In real estate, they want to work with a specific person, so you don't need your logo on everything. You're branding yourself. The more AI and polish we see, the more people look for relatability and human connection, and I'll bet on the person over the company page a thousand out of a thousand times because relatability is all anybody wants. Authentic people with real human connection and real solutions stand out, and that is what your brand ultimately becomes. [BRETT: one line on how this changed how you show up yourself]

**Brand is association, and every moment you show up adds to it or takes from it.**
