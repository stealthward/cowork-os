### Long-form bio

Brett Ward is a business builder and advisor to real estate professionals, based in San Diego. He has been building multimillion-dollar businesses since 2000, starting with a wireless company that grew from one location to eight in about 18 months. In 2009 he founded Loss Mitigation Network, a real estate training and short sale negotiation company that closed over 1,000 short sales. He founded Five Star Escrow in 2011, which has closed over 10,000 real estate transactions, and Real Talk Media in 2012, which has produced over 10,000 real estate videos. Brett talks about how real estate businesses get built and scaled, including content and brand, sales, marketing, and operations. He co-hosts The Factory, a video-first series on the habits and decisions behind a durable business, released on Real Talk Media's channels.
