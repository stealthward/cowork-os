You can't train work ethic, and I spent years trying.
So we stopped building A players and started finding them.

I'd get a spike in performance for a couple of weeks, even a few months, and then they'd go back to their baseline. My ego took a long time to accept that.

The model leg is where we learn their goals and see whether we're a mutual fit.

**When the fit is right, the person grows and the company grows with them.**
