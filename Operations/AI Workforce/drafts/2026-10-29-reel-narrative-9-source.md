## Transcript (verbatim, WhisperX from the finished reel)

In the real estate space, to me, the number one objective of anybody who wants to grow their business is to focus on brand. Two out of every three transactions come by way of referral. That tells you that you have to focus on brand because you're sitting in a chair, you're like, why am I here? Because the other agent didn't do a good job of maintaining their brand in front of that audience. And there are really so much mystery that I think needs to be simplified as it relates to brand and marketing. because there's really only two types of marketing that exist. There's direct response, which is buy now. Coupons, discounts, sales that have expiration dates, all of those. Or there's brand marketing, which is when you're ready to buy, choose me. And as somebody who used to knock on doors, I guarantee you never went up, knocked on a door, and they opened the door like, you know what? We hadn't been thinking about selling our house, but what? Can we fill out a listing agreement right now? Right, exactly.

## Brett's interview answers (2026-10-29, verbatim)

**Q1. When you knocked doors, what did that teach you about direct response versus brand?**
If you've got a strong brand, they'll knock on your door.

**Q2. One thing you've seen an agent do consistently that keeps them in front of past clients?**
They send weekly newsletters with embedded video.

**Q3. Have you seen a past client go to another agent because the first agent disappeared?**
It happens all the time because agents don't have a systematic way to stay in front of past clients.
