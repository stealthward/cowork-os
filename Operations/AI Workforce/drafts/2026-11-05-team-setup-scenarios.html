Team Setup Scenarios

Researched from current Anthropic and GitHub docs on 11/5; the three claims that change the plan were re-checked against the source pages. Nothing is bought or moved. Team launch dates are held until you decide.

The short version

  • Your current setup does not have to change at all. Your account can stay a personal account, outside the Team org. Your OS, seats, skills, chats, and scheduled tasks stay exactly as they are.
  • The Team org can run on two seats: the workforce account as Primary Owner (the one seat Anthropic allows to be a service account) and Elizabeth.
  • Working from any computer is a separate question from Team. The cheapest answer is Remote Control, which drives the office PC from home or your phone. It works on your current plan today.

1. Your account and the Team org

Verified: upgrading an individual account to Team happens in place by default. Your personal subscription is cancelled with a prorated refund, some content moves into the company org, and moved content "can't be moved back." The setup flow has a Keep your personal account separate box that creates a new workspace instead. One email can hold both a personal plan and a Team seat, switching from the account menu.

2. What happens to your local files

  • Nothing moves. Only the scrubbed team kit copy goes to the company's GitHub repo, and the repo sync uses the org's GitHub connection, so members need no GitHub account.
  • Under A, the org kit never reaches your account, so there are no duplicate skills. Under B, plugin skills load as kit:skill-name next to your own; your local skills are unchanged.
  • GitHub: a Free organization covers private repos ($0). Team ($4 per user, $8/month for you and Elizabeth, calc) adds protected branches and required reviewers. GitHub states it does not train AI models on private repo content at rest.
