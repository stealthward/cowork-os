### Conversion Stage
- **Name:** Conversion Stage
- **What it is for:** Definition: "The prospect has made the decision to fix their problem." Objective: offer to fix their problem and encourage them to act now.
- **Parts or steps:**
  1. Definition (above)
  2. Objective (above)
  3. Strategy: clear away last-minute hesitation: a very clear offer, proof of value, and an easy booking path. Push action with urgency, exclusivity, or added value.
  4. Content:
     1. Personalized outreach videos inviting them to schedule a shoot or consultation
     2. Limited-time offers or bonus packages for first-time clients
     3. Video walkthroughs of the booking process to reduce friction
     4. "Getting started" guides that make the first step feel simple
     5. Retargeting ads featuring client testimonials and standout project clips
     6. Social posts celebrating new client partnerships (peer validation)
- **Rules and numbers:** Three levers to push action: urgency, exclusivity, added value. Three jobs: offer crystal clear, proof of value, easy to book.
- **Fits piece type:** (judgment) company-side promo copy, outreach videos, retargeting ads, booking guides. Per Brett's rules, not for personal authority content that gives value first.
- **Copywriter uses it by:** stating the offer plainly, adding proof, and giving one simple next step with one of urgency, exclusivity, or added value.
- **Editor checks:** Is the offer unambiguous and the booking step easy? Is at least one of urgency, exclusivity, or added value present?
- **Source:** Conversion Stage section

### Retention Stage
- **Name:** Retention Stage
- **What it is for:** Definition: the client keeps choosing Real Talk Media and Five Star Escrow for every listing ("increasing the volume of both escrows and media projects").
- **Parts or steps:**
  1. Definition (above)
  2. Objectives:
     1. Maximize escrow volume from each client by making us their default for every transaction
     2. Encourage consistent media usage through credits and rewards
     3. Position Real Talk Media as an indispensable strategic partner, not just a vendor
  3. Strategy:
     1. Maximize escrow capture rate by delivering exceptional media experiences
     2. Use 1:1 communication to reinforce incentives (media credits, Producer's Circle)
     3. Offer proactive strategic marketing ideas that show deep understanding of the agent's business and audience
     4. Make staying easier than leaving, using bundled services, retainer plans, and streamlined scheduling
     5. Showcase their wins (privately and publicly) to validate the relationship and fuel loyalty
  4. Content:
     1. Personalized performance recaps linking closed escrows to earned media credits
     2. Private congratulations & updates on Producer's Circle qualification status
     3. Quarterly strategy sessions (video or in-person) to review marketing goals and present new ideas
     4. Project recaps they can share (with subtle Real Talk branding)
     5. Custom shoot plans tied to their business calendar (e.g., seasonal campaigns, major listings)
     6. Invitations to add retainer-based services with credits offset by their escrow volume
     7. Behind-the-scenes reels featuring their projects, reinforcing prestige and production quality
- **Rules and numbers:** The strategy centers on escrow capture rate (the source names no formal success metric); the definition targets escrow and media volume. Cadence: quarterly strategy sessions. Incentives named: media credits, Producer's Circle, retainer credits offset by escrow volume.
- **Fits piece type:** (judgment) 1:1 client communication, recap videos, strategy-session decks. Company-side only.
- **Copywriter uses it by:** tying the client's own closed results to earned credits or status, and offering a proactive next idea.
- **Editor checks:** Does the piece tie to the client's own wins or business calendar? Is it positioned as a strategic partner, not a vendor pitch?
- **Source:** Retention Stage section
