# Reel narrative 1, v2: Factory Ep 1 R4, three legs of the stool

Building businesses since 2000 has taught us that any successful organization stands on three legs, and when one goes missing, the stool tumbles over.

**Leg one is talent acquisition.** We look for A players, people who come wired with their own personal standards.

We build a team from a group that's aligned, because without one the people who care the most end up covering for the ones who don't. [BRETT: a hire that showed us why the right people matter]

**Leg two is the business model.** An A player needs an opportunity big enough to reach their goals and stay motivated.

If the ceiling is lower than what they want, the best people eventually go find a bigger one. [BRETT: a time the opportunity we offered was too small, or big enough, for someone]

**Leg three is culture.** We want an environment people want to stay in for a long time, because without one we're hiring for a revolving door. [BRETT: a person who stayed, or left, and why]

**Talent acquisition, business model, and culture are what let an organization keep growing and scaling.**
