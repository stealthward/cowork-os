# Operations

## Identity

This workstation covers operations for Five Star Escrow and Real Talk Media: escrow team tooling and dashboards, Apps Script systems, the finance Google Drive structure, CRMLS/Zoho data integrity, process and SOP work, and client operational matters. Route here for anything involving live business systems, team-facing tools, or operational client work. Do not route content production, BD relationships, email drafting, or personal financial matters here.

## Workflow

1. Check MEMORY.md for current priorities and the systems inventory
2. Identify which live system the task touches (dashboard, Apps Script, Drive structure, CRM sync)
3. Read the relevant resource doc before modifying any live system
4. After changes, update the resource doc and MEMORY.md in the same session

## Editorial Rules

Follow my voice principles in 00_Resources (voice-principles.md).

- Any change to a live system (Apps Script, dashboard, Drive structure, CRM configuration) gets documented in the relevant resource doc in the same session. Undocumented changes to team-facing systems are not acceptable.
