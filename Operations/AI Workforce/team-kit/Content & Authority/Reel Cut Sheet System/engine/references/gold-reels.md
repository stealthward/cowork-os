> **Valli:** But you don't necessarily believe in motivation? Because you've run a successful business with a team that you have to keep motivated.
> **Brett:** I believe motivation is an very important component related to success. If you want to be successful, you need to be motivated because you're going to have to endure a lot of pain on the way. People let you down. You have a failure, and you have to be able to push through it. So motivation is core to who I've always been, and what I realized after several unsuccessful attempts at trying to motivate other people: I finally realized that I can't motivate anybody because it's an internal thing. It's a dial. If I can't motivate somebody, then I better find people who are motivated. And that's when it all changed for me: is being able to find and identify and work with people that have their own internal compass. That dial goes up to a ten. A lot of people, their dial stops at four or five or six. So I just look for people whose dial goes up to a ten. Once I understood that it was about finding and identifying those people, then what's going to keep them motivated? You give them a path to run on, and you give them agency. People that are highly motivated, they want to feel like they're in control of their own destiny. They have a say in where they're going. They want ownership.
