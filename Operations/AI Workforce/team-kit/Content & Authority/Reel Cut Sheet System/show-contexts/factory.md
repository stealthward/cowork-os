# The Factory: Project Instructions

This project builds editor-ready reel cut sheets for The Factory podcast using the Reel Cut Sheet skill. For every transcript provided, follow the skill's engine and the Factory Show Context below. Default to a full cut sheet unless the run specifies otherwise.

This is a production project. Engine changes (revisions to the skill, the build script, or the method itself) belong in the Reel Cut Sheet Lab project, not here. If a build reveals an engine problem, note it and take it to the Lab.

## Factory Show Context

- **Hosts / participants:**
  - Brett Ward: founder of Five Star Escrow and Real Talk Media; operator and culture-builder; the connective host who frames each episode.
  - Danny Davis: Encinitas real estate agent and Army veteran; runs a small brokerage but deliberately stays a solo producer built on past-client and referral business.
  - Valli Lopez: 20-year lender and producing agent; long tenure on the financing side of deals.
- **Authority domains (shine zones):**
  - Brett: business operations, systems and SOPs, sales and marketing leadership, real estate team structure and brokerage economics. His credibility is owner-level pattern recognition from running an escrow company with visibility into thousands of transactions and from the media business.
  - Danny: personal brand as the engine of a referral business, the agent-as-brand and lifestyle model, the solo-producer-with-a-support-team approach, and the Encinitas / North County market.
  - Valli: lending and mortgage, the financing side of a transaction, and the long-tenured producing-agent perspective.
- **Audience(s):**
  - Brett serves higher producers, broker-owners, and team leaders: mid-career operators scaling past the ceiling and high earners with no system to support what they've built.
  - Danny serves agents building a personal-brand, referral-driven business without a large team, and his Encinitas / North County sphere.
  - Valli serves agents and clients navigating the lending and financing side, and long-tenured producing agents.
- **Optimization target:** Credibility and authority.
- **Host priority:** None. Each episode is built so each host carries a segment; rank on reel strength and let strong moments distribute across all three.
- **Guardrails:** None named at the show level beyond the engine's standard approval and rights categories. All three hosts run active businesses and reference real transactions and clients, so client-identifying detail, live deal specifics, and third-party personal stories are handled under those categories on a per-episode basis.
- **Reel count target:** None. The built set self-sizes on tier.
- **Capture:** Multicam with a per-participant isolated angle, so reaction shots and cutaways of the other hosts are always available.
- **Segment structure / theme:** A business and life show with real estate fluency, built for ambitious professionals and the consumers they serve. Each episode runs three segments; one host leads each from their own domain while the other two weigh in. The throughline is three people at three stations in life, and the contrast and tension between them is the show.
