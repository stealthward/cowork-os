## Product 1: List It Live

- Description: Interview style talk show for agents to showcase their listings
- Primary Benefits: Quality content at scale (get four professional videos); Save time (takes less than 30 minutes); Get massive exposure for your listing and your brand; Impress your clients and prospects; Tool to win new listings; It's very easy
- Target Pain Points Solved: Content creation feels too time-consuming or expensive. No desire to learn technical production skills. Competitors dominate market share through consistent content. People forget you sell real estate, loss of referrals and repeat business. Lack of brand recognition and strong online presence. No proof of success, perceived inactivity or inexperience. Not seen as an expert or thought leader. Referral partners have no content to share. Missed organic search opportunities. Fewer social touchpoints and engagements. Lack of differentiation in a crowded market.
- Best Funnel Stage Fit: Awareness, Consideration, Conversion
- Proof Points: Over 5,000 properties featured, 10,000+ views on every episode, favorite marketing tool for top producers
- Example Messaging Lines: "Showcase your listings in front of thousands of viewers across every major platform." "We make it easy to create polished content designed to impress clients and generate more listings." "Join us for 30 minutes to get 4 custom videos to showcase your listing and elevate your brand." "We'll make you shine on camera and deliver your videos in one day."
