## Awareness stage

- Definition: the prospect becomes aware of a problem that is preventing them from getting what they want.
- Objectives: make prospects aware they have a problem and show them how life will improve when it is fixed.
- Strategy: make the pain point feel urgent and important enough that they start seeking solutions. Use examples of peer success to spark FOMO and curiosity.

## Consideration stage

- Definition: the prospect begins exploring ways to fix their problem.
- Objective: demonstrate that we provide the most effective solution to their problem.
- Strategy: educate prospects on what makes an effective real estate media strategy, then prove Real Talk Media delivers it better, faster, and easier than alternatives. Remove uncertainty by showing process clarity, quality outcomes, and client satisfaction.

## Conversion stage

- Definition: the prospect has made the decision to fix their problem.
- Objective: offer to fix their problem and encourage them to act now.
- Strategy: remove any final hesitation by making the offer crystal clear, showing proof of value, and making it easy to book. Use urgency, exclusivity, or added value to push action.

## Retention stage

- Definition: the client continues to choose Real Talk Media, and Five Star Escrow, for every listing, increasing the volume of both escrows and media projects.
- Objectives: maximize escrow volume from each client by making us their default for every transaction; encourage consistent media usage through credits and rewards; position Real Talk Media as an indispensable strategic partner, not just a vendor.
- Strategy: maximize escrow capture rate by delivering exceptional media experiences; use 1:1 communication to reinforce incentives (media credits, Producer's Circle); offer proactive, strategic marketing ideas that demonstrate deep understanding of the agent's business and audience; make it easier for clients to keep working with us than to go anywhere else, through bundled services, retainer plans, and streamlined scheduling; showcase their wins (privately and publicly) to validate the relationship and fuel loyalty.
