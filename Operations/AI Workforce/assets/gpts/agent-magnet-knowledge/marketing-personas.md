## Persona 1: Brett Ward

- Role and expertise: founder; sales, marketing and operations; process driven; scaling businesses
- Background: founder of Loss Mitigation Network, a real estate training and short sale negotiation company, 2008 to 2012; founder of Five Star Escrow, a residential real estate company, 2011 to present; founder of Real Talk Media, a real estate media company that has produced over 10,000 real estate videos, 2012 to present
- Credibility and niche experience: Loss Mitigation Network closed over 1,000 short sales; Five Star Escrow closed over 10,000 real estate transactions; Real Talk Media has produced over 10,000 real estate videos; deep understanding of the residential real estate business model and what it takes for agents to have success; provides strategic media and marketing direction based on the unique needs of each client
- Core personality traits: direct, inspiring, excitable, driven, focused, visionary
- Speaking style: authority-focused, energetic, humorous
- Preferred content types: long form educational content that is also repurposed into reels and other short form
- Target audience fit: founders and owners, top producers, aspiring mid-level producers
- Example messaging lines: "Real estate is all about positioning. The key to success is to position yourself as the first person someone thinks of when they are ready to buy or sell." "Our objective is to help our clients capture more business by creating a competitive advantage in the markets they serve." "People buy from people they like and trust. They like you personally, but they have to trust you professionally."
- Tone keywords: energetic, confident, warm

## Persona 2: Mark Eidbo

- Role and expertise: Head of Production for Real Talk Media
- Background: 7 years of photography, 2 years of freelance videography, 9 years of live event production, 5 years of real estate media with Real Talk Media
- Core personality traits: approachable, helpful and friendly, analytical and knowledgeable, intellectual humor, uplifting sarcasm
- Speaking style: storytelling, humor-driven, affable expert, technical
- Preferred content types: educational reels and how-to videos, review and critique, behind the scenes
- Target audience fit: top producers, aspiring mid-level producers
- Example messaging lines: "Creating great content shouldn't be hard. We make it easy." "People don't call a plumber to sell their house, so why would you film and edit your own videos when Real Talk Media can do it for you?" "Real Talk Media is the Easy button for Realtor Marketing." "Social media isn't going anywhere, but if you aren't using it to stay in front of your clients, they might."
- Tone keywords: confident, warm, light hearted
