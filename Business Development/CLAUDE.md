# Business Development

## Identity

This workstation covers revenue-generating relationship activity: target identification and research, personal outreach to high producers, strategic partnership development, referral partner management, pipeline tracking, and converting relationships into Five Star escrow commitments. BD strategy is content-driven: the shows and Brett's personal content create the authority and conversation starters, and BD converts that attention into relationships and escrow volume. Route here for prospect or partner outreach, meeting prep, follow-up strategy, pipeline review, and anything involving converting RTM relationships to Five Star escrow. Do not route show production, episode guest research, content planning, general operations, financial oversight, or internal team management here.

**The Rebuild seam:** Rebuild guests are often prospects. Content & Authority owns everything about the episode: guest research for the show, briefs, the shoot, and the content package. This workstation owns the relationship: target selection, pre-show and post-show outreach, and escrow conversion.

## Workflow

**Prospect to Partnership**
1. Identify target: $50M+ list-side producers, high-volume teams, brokerages, or investor groups
2. Research their business and digital footprint
3. Open the relationship: warm intro through a referral partner, a Rebuild guest invitation, or direct personal outreach
4. Build the relationship through content value and advisory conversations. No direct pitch.
5. The ask is always an escrow commitment to Five Star, never a media sale
6. Escrow volume funds RTM content production at no cost to the client. Retainers exist only when content demand exceeds what escrow volume supports. No one-off paid projects.

## Editorial Rules

Follow Brett's voice principles in 00_Resources (voice-principles.md).

- Prospect emails lead with insight about their specific business, not a pitch
- Frame the opportunity, not just the problem
- CC the introducing referral partner on communications with prospects they introduced
- Never pitch Five Star directly in early RTM conversations. Let the relationship build first.
- CTAs are always specific: a meeting, a call, a review. Never vague next steps.
