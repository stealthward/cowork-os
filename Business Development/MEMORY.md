## BD Model (Current)

BD is content-driven and runs in two phases (Brett, 2026-09-23): build visibility, credibility, and authority while giving value, then switch to acquisition mode aimed at each avatar. Business arrives in large tranches through two channels: the investor community (REInvest) and the FSE partner campaign (the county's highest producers, teams, and brokerages). The path:

1. In-house content builds authority: The Factory (training wheels), Rebuild with Brett hosting by 2027-03-01 (the destination), Brett's direct-to-camera content teaching REACH principles and operator-level content on sales, marketing, and operations
2. Relationships open through referral partner intros, Rebuild guest invitations, or direct personal outreach; no primary door. Rebuild guests: owner-operators of investment firms, high-producing agents, team leaders, brokers, adjacent vendors with agent relationships, potential referral partners
3. Value builds through content and advisory conversations, never a direct pitch. Brett's relationship-building content gives first; company content keeps its calls to action and quote pitches
4. The close is always an escrow commitment to Five Star. The media value exchange is always the preferred close, including for top producers. For the ones media alone cannot capture, the Five Star partner structure opens: a Five Star partner is a Five Star employee and legally earns commissions on escrow. Never call it revenue share. (Brett, 2026-09-24) Media is the incentive, not the product. Retainers exist only when content demand exceeds what escrow volume supports. No one-off paid projects.
