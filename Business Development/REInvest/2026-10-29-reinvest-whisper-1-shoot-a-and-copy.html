Shoot A shot list

A sizzle reel: the room builds itself, one element per beat (Mark and Brett, 2026-10-29). Every beat uses the same locked-off frame, so each state cuts to the next as if it popped into place. About 90 minutes on site (the task's estimate). Vertical 9:16 is the primary frame; if a second camera is available, run a horizontal one beside it for the partner whisper kit due 11/12.

Camera

  • Tripod, locked off, from the back of the room facing the front, so the TV, the table position, the whiteboard position, and all four rows fit in frame. Nobody touches the camera between beats.
  • Manual exposure, focus, and white balance, set for the fully lit room and left alone. The opening reads dark because the lights are off, not because the camera changes.
  • Roll 5 to 10 seconds on each state with the room clear. Crew and furniture move only between takes, all of it out of frame.

Keep off camera

  • The event date or month, the event name ("Find the Deal"), and the 50.
  • Any speaker name, photo, or company. No partner logos, signage, or branded cups anywhere in frame.
  • People. The room is empty in every beat.
  • Whiteboard content: it stays blank, so there's nothing to blur.

Before the shoot

  • Confirm The Hive's TV can show the REInvest logo from a laptop or USB drive. The light and dark PNGs aren't in the REInvest folder yet, so production exports the logo from the Canva logo file (DAHGUTzzjxM, color option 2).
  • Find the one light that clicks on for beat 2 and test that it lights part of the room, not all of it.
  • Stage the four rows of chairs, the whiteboard, and the speaker's table just out of frame, in build order.
