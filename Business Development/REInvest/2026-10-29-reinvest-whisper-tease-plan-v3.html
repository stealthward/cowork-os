How the campaign works

  • Hormozi's launch method, verified in $100M Leads (affiliates chapter, Step 5). A Whisper creates curiosity: short, behind the scenes of building the thing, ending on "not yet." A Tease reveals the product, the date, and the elements of value (What, Who, When).
  • His cadence, counted back from 2/18: whisper every 4 to 6 weeks until 60 days out, every 2 to 3 weeks until 30 days out, then tease weekly until 14 days out and twice weekly until 3 days out.
  • Partners do the advertising. The book files launches under affiliates for a reason: do the work for them so they can plug and play. Each partner and presenter gets the Partner whisper kit (due 11/12) and posts between our beats.
  • Quality over quantity. Ten anchor pieces from us, each one strong, instead of daily volume.
