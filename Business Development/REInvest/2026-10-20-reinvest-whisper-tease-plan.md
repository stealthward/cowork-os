## Direction (Brett, 2026-10-20, after v2; supersedes v2 where they differ)

- Quality over quantity. The campaign builds awareness and brand recognition and creates buzz in San Diego's investor community, mainly through curiosity and recognizable partners and presenters.
- Reach runs through the partners' and presenters' social accounts and email lists, not mainly through RTM and Five Star.
- Expected demand: 300+ email addresses for 50 seats. Seats go by personal invitation from partners and presenters. Real estate agents are invited too; Brett has thousands of agent emails in Zoho, including the top agents.
- Goal: people asking the partners and presenters how to get in. Demand beats supply, and the supply limit is real (the 50-seat cap), so it is shown through mechanism, never claimed.
- Brett is rethinking topics and platforms. The ten Asana subtasks stay generic, with the v2 topics in their descriptions as suggestions (written 2026-10-20). The table below is a suggestion set, not a decision.

## What the research changed

- **Concrete beats coy.** ARV's best event post (88 likes, 14 comments) was a single image with date, time, and place; a name-only teaser drew 4. Teases name specifics.
- **Recaps carry the reach.** VREV's recap carousel (78 likes) and reel (2,728 plays) ran 1 to 4 days after its event. A recap after 2/18 is outside the ten; it goes in the Content & Authority production handoff (1/21).
- **Partners do part of the reach.** Co-hosts and community accounts tagged and reposted ARV's events; no collab posts were used. The partner kit (12/17) carries Instagram-ready assets.
- **The most active flippers are mostly not on Instagram.** Of the 49 top flipping entities (Opendoor excluded), 7 have a confirmed account, 5 of them under 1,000 followers, and few posted in the last 90 days (item 4 brief). Instagram reaches the community around the events (agents, lenders, community accounts); the flippers themselves are reached by email (84 of 153 flipping entities) and through partners.
- **Our accounts are small and quiet** (RTM averages 7.9 interactions over its last 12 posts, Five Star 3.8), so tagging partners matters more than posting volume.
