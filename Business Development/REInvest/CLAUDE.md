# REInvest

## Project Identity

REInvest is an invitation-only quarterly workshop series for serious residential real estate investors, produced by Real Talk Media at The Hive and capped at 50 seats. It belongs to Business Development. Three objectives: new client acquisition, client retention, and content. End state for this project: founding table secured, Partner tier recruited, and the first event (REInvest: Find the Deal, 2027-02-18) executed. External tracker: the [REInvest project in Asana](https://app.asana.com/1/459051476492004/project/1218246612327620).

## Seams

- **Investor Database (sibling project):** the targeting engine. Invitation lists come from registry queries (flip activity, per-partner relationships, reachable contacts). Every event pushes new contacts and relationship confirmations back into the registry.
- **Content & Authority:** owns event production, capture, and reel packages once production planning starts, the same split as the Rebuild seam. This project owns partners, the room, and escrow conversion.

## Project Rules

- **Compliance:** never name or reference Greg Ives or First American Title in any REInvest material, and no partner logos anywhere. Greg operates as a founding partner without the title, in one-to-one conversations only.
- Banned in names and copy: "system," "playbook," "wealth" (as branding), "ecosystem," "learn" (in event names).
- Deep over wide: two experts per event covering different pillars of one topic, occasionally three. Never a wide panel.
- The "what's in it for you" psychology is addressed in narrative, never printed as a label. Guests are third person in partner-facing materials; "you/your" is reserved for the partner's own role.
- Scarcity is shown through mechanism, never claimed. Seats cannot be bought; they are offered by personal invitation from partners and presenters. Anyone may request to be considered through the waitlist form, and a request joins the waitlist, never a seat directly (Brett, 2026-10-22). No open registration ever; the cap stays at 50 and demand creates a waitlist, not a bigger venue.
- Invitees may be told the real facts plainly: the 50-seat cap, their reply-by date, and that a waitlist exists. Live seat counts ("two seats left") and pressure language are never used. (Brett, 2026-09-29)
- Experts are never called sponsors. Sponsorship (Five Star anchor plus two external max, no stage time) is a separate track.
