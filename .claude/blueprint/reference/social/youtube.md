# Social audit: YouTube

_Platform file for the mandatory social content audit. Read with social-audit.md (shared core, window, cost, output). Built 2026-10-14._

**Role in the brand system** (Content Authority & Social Media Playbook 15): search-based authority, process education, deeper trust.

## Pull

| Data | Actor | Listed price (2026-10-14) |
|---|---|---|
| Channel videos and Shorts (title, description, date, duration, views, likes, comments) | streamers/youtube-scraper | $0.004 per video |
| Transcripts (top three long-form videos by observed views only) | add-on per social-audit.md | $0.048 per minute |

Watch time and retention are private to the channel owner; never infer them.

## What to look for

| # | Check | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Channel identity | Channel name, banner, and about state service plus geography; links to the website |
| 2 | Search-style topics | Titles answer what people in the market search: neighborhoods, moving to [City], selling in [market], costs, process |
| 3 | Titles and thumbnails | Titles hit at least two headline components (content-frameworks.md 4.3); thumbnails show a face and a readable promise |
| 4 | Long-form vs Shorts | The mix, and observed views per format; Shorts that are clips of long-form vs standalone |
| 5 | Opening | In the transcribed videos, the first 30 seconds state the payoff instead of warming up |
| 6 | Process education | Videos that show how the agent works (pricing, prep, negotiation) build trust before contact |
| 7 | Organization | Playlists by audience or neighborhood; consistent series |
| 8 | Calls to action | A clear next step in the video and description that matches the website CTA |

Failure modes to name: listing tours with no narration, uploads with default titles, a channel abandoned after a burst, view counts carried by one old video.
