# Social audit: LinkedIn

_Platform file for the mandatory social content audit. Read with social-audit.md (shared core, window, cost, output). Built 2026-10-14._

**Role in the brand system** (Content Authority & Social Media Playbook 15): professional credibility, referral network visibility, relocation or executive audience reach.

## Pull

| Data | Actor | Listed price (2026-10-14) |
|---|---|---|
| Profile posts (text, images, documents, video, reposts) | harvestapi/linkedin-profile-posts (no cookies) | $0.002 per post |

The profile itself (headline, About, experience) is read by the sidekick from the public profile page or, if blocked, an Apify profile actor chosen at run time and logged.

## What to look for

| # | Check | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline | Role, market, and specialty; not just "Realtor at [Brokerage]" |
| 2 | About | First person, who they serve, proof, how they work; consistent with portal bios |
| 3 | Original vs reposts | Original posts carry the account; reposts and brokerage shares are a minority |
| 4 | Post types | Text-first and document posts with a point of view; native video; not only listing announcements |
| 5 | Hooks | The first two lines (before "see more") hit at least two headline components (content-frameworks.md 4.3) |
| 6 | Who engages | Commenters are peers, lenders, attorneys, past clients, referral partners (read names and titles on top posts), not only other agents congratulating |
| 7 | Professional authority | Market insight, process, deal lessons, client stories with permission |
| 8 | Referral visibility | Content that keeps the agent top of mind with people who refer: recognition of partners, relocation and executive relevance |

Failure modes to name: an abandoned profile, a headline that names only the brokerage, cross-posted Instagram captions with hashtags, no comments from anyone outside real estate.

75/25 on LinkedIn: the audience-building side is professional and community insight, not lifestyle; judge the balance against this role, not Instagram's.
