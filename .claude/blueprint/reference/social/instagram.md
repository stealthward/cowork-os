# Social audit: Instagram

_Platform file for the mandatory social content audit. Read with social-audit.md (shared core, window, cost, output). Built 2026-10-14._

**Role in the brand system** (Content Authority & Social Media Playbook 15): attention, local lifestyle relevance, light authority, familiarity.

## Pull

| Data | Actor | Listed price (2026-10-14) |
|---|---|---|
| Profile (bio, link, follower and post counts, highlights titles) | apify/instagram-profile-scraper | $0.0026 per profile |

Stories are not pulled (they expire and are not public history); note highlight titles only.

## What to look for

| # | Check | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Profile | Name field carries service plus geography; bio says who they serve and where; link goes to a page that matches; highlights organized (listings, reviews, neighborhoods, about) |
| 2 | Grid first impression | The last 9 to 12 posts read as one agent in one market, not a flyer wall |
| 3 | Reels share and hooks | Reels are a meaningful share of output; the first three seconds and on-screen text hit at least two headline components (content-frameworks.md 4.3) |
| 4 | Reels vs static engagement | Observed views and likes per format; which format the audience rewards |
| 5 | Local lifestyle content | Neighborhoods, businesses, events: the 75 side of 75/25 |
| 6 | On-camera presence | The agent appears in person, not only listing photos and graphics |
| 7 | Captions | First line earns the "more" tap; location named; no hashtag walls standing in for substance |
| 8 | Collaboration | Collab posts and tags with local businesses (Sphere of Proximity) |

Failure modes to name: listing-flyer reposts, brokerage templates, motivational quote graphics, long gaps.
