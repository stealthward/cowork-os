## The product

The Real Estate Branding Blueprint: delivered strategy for signed $50M+ clients (growth-strategy.md), not a closer. It turns an agent's public footprint, search visibility, and discovery context into a phased plan the agent can execute.

## The spine: three phases, strict separation

1. **Diagnose.** Internal Brand Audit (blunt, internal, with the mandatory social content audit), then Brett adds his manual analysis, then the RTM Digital Brand Analysis, Discovery Meeting Agenda, and Cover Email. Diagnostic only: what the footprint communicates, what works, what is unclear or inconsistent, what discovery must resolve.
2. **Discover.** Brett runs the discovery meeting and supplies the summary: the real business model, differentiators, ideal client, goals for 12 to 24 months, channel priorities, lead sources, desired perception.
3. **Strategize.** Real Estate Branding Blueprint plus Cover Email, then the QA pass. Execution copy (portal bios, site copy) follows as Phase 1 work.

Never skip a step. Never introduce strategy before the Blueprint.

## What each step may and may not contain

| Step | May contain | May not contain |
|---|---|---|
| Internal Brand Audit | Blunt findings, tactical notes for Brett, what needs manual review, a prompt for his manual analysis | A blueprint, final positioning, rewrites |
| RTM Digital Brand Analysis | Narrative diagnosis, restrained copy examples that prove a flaw or contrast weak with stronger language | Final recommendations, final positioning, full rewrites, site architecture, a content roadmap, blueprint language |
| Discovery Meeting Agenda | Short, conversation-friendly prompts | Answers or assumptions about the client |
| Real Estate Branding Blueprint | Strategy, structured assets, phased plan, Closing Direction | Technical SEO, a content calendar, a project-management task list |

## Narrative progression (Analysis and Blueprint)

Where you are now; the key problems and misalignments; what needs to change; the stronger position you can achieve.

## Tone by step

| Step | Tone |
|---|---|
| Internal Brand Audit | Internal, efficient, blunt. Challenge weak positioning |
| RTM Digital Brand Analysis | Client-facing, diagnostic, clear, non-abrasive, polished narrative |
| Real Estate Branding Blueprint | Client-facing, strategic, conclusive, aspirational, manageable |

All steps: direct, specific, no hype, no generic realtor adjectives, no em dashes. Prefer direct assertion over "It is not X. It is Y." constructions.

## Implementation bias

Clarity over complexity, sequence over overwhelm, practical execution over theoretical completeness. Every step leaves Brett a clear next step.
