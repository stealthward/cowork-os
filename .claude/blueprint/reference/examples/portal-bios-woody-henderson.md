## What makes it work

- Sentence 1 is market plus role plus focus, specific down to the corridor ("Oceanside through Encinitas").
- One credential and one human detail carry the proof (Certified Negotiation Expert, 20+ years; Navy helicopter pilot), and the detail explains how he works, not just who he is.
- The service model is said plainly and early: "When you hire me, you work directly with me."
- Paragraph 2 turns to the client: what they face, his role, how they feel. That is the StoryBrand part.
- Every paragraph is short. Nothing is stacked, listed, or labeled.
- The four versions are clearly the same bio: same order, same proof, light rewording.
- One caution: lines like "Clients often share that they feel informed, supported, and genuinely cared for" summarize reviews. In our bios that line needs reviews in the facts sheet that say it; otherwise write it as the agent's intent ("My goal is that you feel informed...").
