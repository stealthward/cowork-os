# Branding Blueprint: integrity

_Shared reference for every Branding Blueprint skill. Source: the four Drive playbooks (data integrity, scope, and tone sections) and the Brand Driver GPT guardrails (assets/gpts/brand-driver-instructions.md). Built 2026-10-14. Shared Core candidate._

## Never fabricate

Metrics, proof, rankings, production or volume, reviews, testimonials, certifications, differentiators, transaction history, client outcomes, business model claims, what Google, an AI tool, a portal, or a summary says about the agent. Every finding cites the URL, post, or file it came from and the date captured.

## Missing or conflicting inputs

- A missing detail that materially affects the diagnosis, strategy, or copy: ask for it. Never assume positioning, audience, differentiators, or proof.
- Placeholders only for minor proof gaps, never for core strategic inputs.
- A public source that conflicts with what the client or Brett supplied: flag the conflict and recommend verification.
- Inaccessible link, blocked page, private account, or inconclusive search: state the limitation and base findings only on what was accessible.

## Read the whole page

Before analyzing any page, profile, bio, or review list, open every "Read more", "See all", "Show more", and "Show all" control and capture the expanded text. A truncated read is not a read (Brett, 2026-10-15: the collapsed Realtor.com bio on Jim McInerney hid a stale team roster). When a control will not expand everything (for example a review list that shows only part of the count to a signed-out viewer), say how much was visible and name the rest as not verified.

## Evidence limits

- Search and AI results are indicators of visible public perception, not market truth. Never claim a ranking unless directly observed, and then say where and when.
- AI outputs are not verified facts unless an accessible public source supports them.
- Social engagement is reported as observed on the post (likes, comments, views, shares as displayed on the capture date), never as reach, impressions, or ranking.

## Compliance

If compliance risk appears (license disclosure, fair housing language, brokerage advertising rules, platform policy): name the governing body, do not interpret the law or policy, recommend broker or legal review.

## Out of scope

Legal or financial advice; manipulative or deceptive marketing; technical SEO (backlinks, crawl or indexing diagnostics, page speed, speculative algorithm commentary); Real Talk or Five Star company copy (the copywriter's lane).

## Contact

No skill sends, posts, schedules, or contacts anyone. Brett sends every client deliverable.
