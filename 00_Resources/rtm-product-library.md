## How clients get the products (Brett, 2026-09-24)

- Every product is exclusive to Five Star clients. There is no way to buy them without an escrow relationship.
- A credit system based on the number of escrows closed lets a client use the products at no cost. The amount and frequency of media a client gets are set by the escrows they close.
- Monthly retainers exist for agents who want more media than their escrow volume earns and want it on a consistent basis. Recurring podcasts and recurring on-location shoots fall under retainers.
- The credit system and its thresholds are reinforced one to one and never stated publicly (standing rule).

## The problem every product answers

The Doc maps each product to the same list of agent pain points (from persona 2 in audience-personas.md): competitors dominate through consistent content; people forget the agent sells real estate; no brand recognition; no proof of success; not seen as an expert; referral partners have nothing to share; missed search and success-story opportunities; too few touchpoints; no differentiation; and the fears (where to start, overwhelm, topics, camera, time, cost, not wanting to learn production). Choose the product by which of those the agent named.

## Positioning rules that travel with the products

- Media is the incentive, not the product. The close is always an escrow commitment to Five Star. Retainers exist only when content demand exceeds what escrow volume supports. No one-off paid projects.
- Retention products are why a client sends every listing through Five Star; incentives behind them (media credits, Producer's Circle) are reinforced one to one and never stated publicly.
- Prioritize time savings, strategic guidance, listing growth, top-of-mind awareness, and differentiation over "pro-level video" claims.
- No consumer-facing content for agents; peer success examples only.

### Product 6: San Diego Market Movers

- Description: An extension of List It Live. Every agent who appears on List It Live is interviewed on San Diego Market Movers immediately after, on any topic they choose. Designed for personal branding above and beyond a listing.
- Primary Benefits: Get interviewed on the topic you want to be known for, in the same studio visit as your listing; Build your personal brand, not just your listing's; Add an expert-positioning video to every listing cycle; No extra prep or production on your side
- Target Pain Points Solved: Not seen as an expert or thought leader. Lack of brand recognition and strong online presence. Lack of differentiation in a crowded market. Fewer social touchpoints and engagements. Unsure what topics to cover
- Best Funnel Stage Fit: Consideration, Conversion
- Proof Points: None on file yet
- Example Messaging Lines: "Your listing gets List It Live. You get San Diego Market Movers." "Thirty minutes in the studio, two shows, one stronger brand." "Talk about what you want to be known for, and we'll put it in front of the market."

### Product 7: Agent Brand Videos

- Description: A story-driven brand video that builds an authentic connection with prospects before they ever meet you. Your reputation deserves more than a headshot.
- Primary Benefits: Become the obvious choice; Turn your story into a marketing asset; More connection, more credibility, more listings; One evergreen piece that works on your website, social, and listing presentations
- Target Pain Points Solved: Lack of brand recognition and strong online presence. Lack of differentiation in a crowded market. Failure to build trust with potential clients. Fear of looking foolish on camera
- Best Funnel Stage Fit: Consideration
- Proof Points: Melissa Tucci spotlight
- Example Messaging Lines: "More connection, more credibility, more listings." "Become the obvious choice before the first meeting." "Your story is the marketing asset you have not used yet."

### Product 8: Real Talk Studios

- Description: A 2,500 square foot studio with seven sets (Lobby, Set 1, Set 2, Green Screen, Podcast Table, Events and Seminars), an immersive video wall in the lobby, two talk show sets, a green screen room, and The Hive for seminars, panels, and live-audience shows. The venue behind every Real Talk product.
- Primary Benefits: Look like the professional you are; Every product is produced in a purpose-built space, not on a phone; Host your own seminar, panel, or live-audience show; Walk in, sit down, and record
- Target Pain Points Solved: No desire to learn technical production skills. Content creation feels too time-consuming or expensive. Fear of looking foolish on camera. Lack of differentiation in a crowded market
- Best Funnel Stage Fit: Consideration, Conversion; the studio tour is the conversion step
- Proof Points: Studio tour
- Example Messaging Lines: "Look like the professional you are. Because anyone can do phone videos." "Seven sets, one visit, every piece of content you need."
