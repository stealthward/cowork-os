# Growth strategy: 12 to 24 months

_Personal Layer. Brett's macro vision for how the companies grow, dictated 2026-09-23 and rounded out by four clarifying questions the same day. Read before any business development, content strategy, show planning, guest selection, or outreach work. Targets and finish lines live in initiatives.md; the who-we-serve detail lives in the persona files once written. This file is the why and the shape._

---

## The sequence

Two phases, in order. Phase 2 does not start until phase 1 has done its work.

1. **Build visibility, credibility, and authority, and provide value.** Delivered through the media platforms below. Direct-to-camera content teaches the principles in REACH (Real Estate Agent Content Handbook) once it is launched, to build credibility. Alongside it, operator-level content on every part of building and scaling a business: sales, marketing, operations. Both are meant to deliver value to the target audience, not to sell.
2. **Transition into acquisition mode.** When authority and goodwill are established, deploy acquisition strategies targeted at each avatar through the two channels.

The workforce build order follows this: content agents first, acquisition tooling second.

## The thesis

Business arrives in large tranches through two channels. Neither one is worked by selling.

| Channel | Who | Vehicle | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investor community | Current investor clients, and real estate agents who are investors or want to be | REInvest (quarterly, invitation-only) and the content around it | Acquisition of new investors and retention of the ones Five Star already has. Five Star's reputation and its largest, highest-volume investor clients are the head start |
| Partner campaign | The county's highest producers, teams, and brokerages | Mixed doors: referral partner intros, Brett's direct outreach, Rebuild guest invitations. No primary door | The Real Talk value exchange is always the first and preferred close, and many top producers commit on that alone. For those who cannot be captured through media, the Five Star partner structure opens: a Five Star partner is a Five Star employee, so they legally earn commissions on escrow. Media presence makes every door easier to open |

Two birds, one stone, in both channels: REInvest's investor partners deliver the content, and the marketing reaches both the investment community and higher-producing agents. Rebuild does the same with a different mix.

## The mechanism: reversed sales psychology

Brett does not sell to either community. He gives value until they come to him: goodwill, exposure, and recognition first (the Hormozi give-first playbook). What they come for is the combination of who Brett is, what he produces, what Real Talk produces, and what Five Star can execute. The ask for a decision comes at the appropriate time, and Five Star and Real Talk content still carries calls to action and quote pitches where they belong; give-first governs Brett's relationship-building, not every piece of company content.

**Terms.** Never use "revenue share" or "rev share" for the Five Star partner structure. The correct description: a Five Star partner is a Five Star employee and legally earns commissions on escrow. The partner structure is a channel strategy matter, not a Max Income matter; Max Income is Brett's personal finance and tax work only, and it reads from the businesses one way.

## The credibility source

Brett as an operator, not a producer: building multimillion-dollar businesses since 2000 (the first was a wireless company that went from one location to eight in about 18 months) and executed well north of 10,000 videos through Real Talk Media. Both halves matter. The top producers need to see a competent business operator and someone who can provide value through media. That requires Brett out in front, doing as much quality media as possible, instead of behind the scenes.

## The content ladder

1. **The Factory** is the training wheels: camera reps and comfort, with content dripping now for exposure.
2. **Rebuild**, with Brett as host, is the destination. Target: Brett hosting by 2027-03-01. Rebuild build-out resumes to hit that date.
3. **What happens to The Factory** once Rebuild is real is undecided. One option Brett is considering: he steps out as host and rotates in current clients and prospects who are mid-tier producers. That makes The Factory the vehicle for the second avatar.
4. **REACH, the Real Estate Agent Content Handbook** (written manuscript complete 2026-12-18, launch by end of Q1 2027), is the content engine behind all of it and, once complete, a high-value lead magnet. It is offered only after a lot of content built on its principles has gone out. The funnel around it is below.

## The REACH funnel (captured 2026-09-24)

Brett's design, in his order. Making REACH too easy to get would forfeit data and opportunity, so the scorecard is the front door.

1. **Scorecard one (ScoreApp) is the entry.** It exposes the holes and weaknesses in an agent's content and brand strategy. Completing it captures the data.
2. **REACH is the reward.** The handbook provides the answers to what the scorecard exposed.
3. **The agent chooses.** Execute the content strategies on their own, or engage Real Talk. **Scorecard two** is positioned at the engage-Real-Talk step.
4. **The Real Estate Branding Blueprint** (produced by the Branding Blueprint skills) keeps its three parts from the original spec: digital footprint analysis, gap report, custom plan. Brett wants the output redesigned: higher quality, more engaging, less copy-dense. **Decided (Brett, 2026-09-24):** the Blueprint is the content strategy Real Talk executes for $50M+ clients after they sign, not an acquisition closer. The media value exchange stays the close; the Blueprint is what a signed client receives.

Timing: content on REACH principles goes out first (Q4 2026 on), the written manuscript completes 2026-12-18, REACH launches by end of Q1 2027, then the funnel opens. The Branding Blueprint skills have to be ready by the time it does.

## Rebuild: the guest mix

Rebuild highlights every part of real estate and its adjacent verticals. Guest categories:

- Owner-operators of investment firms
- High-producing agents, team leaders, brokers
- Adjacent vendors and verticals with relationships to agents
- Potential referral partners

The value to a guest is exposure and a content package, an introduction to the studio, Real Talk, and Brett. For partner-campaign prospects it is the door; for referral partners it is the relationship; for investors it overlaps with REInvest.

## The three avatars

| Avatar | Who | Brett's involvement | Vehicle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top producers | $50M+ list-side producers, large teams, brokerages | Brett pursues personally | Partner campaign, Rebuild guest invitations, the Real Talk value exchange first; the Five Star partner structure only for those media alone cannot capture |
| Mid-tier producers | Current clients and prospects at 8+ list sides a year; good-to-great Five Star clients | Brett does not pursue; marketing attracts them, the team gathers them | Client marketing framework (RTM captures, Five Star monetizes); possibly The Factory with rotating hosts |
| Investors | Current investor clients; agents who are or want to be investors | Brett hosts the room | REInvest events and associated content, delivered by investor partners |

## What this means for the workforce

- The copywriter and editor agents exist to put Brett in front with quality media at volume. Brett's relationship-building content is give-first; a draft in his voice that pitches Five Star or Real Talk to producers, partners, or investors fails the eval. Company content keeps its calls to action and quote pitches.
- The research sidekick serves guest selection across the four Rebuild categories, REInvest partner and attendee research, and partner-campaign prospecting.
- The Zoho contact data agent keeps the three avatars and the guest categories clean in the CRM.
