# Five Star Escrow Brand Summary

_Source: 2026 Five Star Escrow Brand Kit.pdf_

## Tagline

"Your key to a stress-free closing."

## Core Values

- **Integrity** — Every transaction handled with honesty, transparency, and professionalism to protect all parties.
- **Communication** — Clear, consistent updates keep clients informed and confident throughout every stage.
- **Expertise** — Decades of experience and deep industry knowledge ensure every detail is managed with precision and care.

## Brand Statement

"Five Star isn't just our name, it's our philosophy. Every detail matters — from communication and compliance to the client experience at closing. The Five Star Standard means exceeding expectations, building trust, and ensuring every transaction is handled with precision and care."

## Typography

**Logo:**
- Cormorant (serif) — headers. Conveys trust and timeless sophistication.
- Lexend Deca (sans-serif) — subcopy. Ensures legibility and contemporary feel.

**Marketing Materials:**
- Linux Libertine (serif) — headers. Adds authority and warmth.
- TT Norms (sans-serif) — subcopy and body text. Modern, neutral, highly readable.

All fonts available via Google Fonts or Dafont.com.

## Logo

Primary logo: refined typography paired with a stylized star accented by a blue number 5. Combines clean serif and sans-serif letterforms. Secondary logo and favicon variants exist.

## Imagery Style

- High-resolution, clear, visually engaging
- Positive and approachable tone — people, properties, moments that feel authentic and uplifting
- Avoid stock photos with identifiable faces
- Color tone: balanced, neutral to cool, lean slightly blue to align with brand palette
- Avoid heavy yellow or warm tints

## Brand Design Principles

- **Clean geometric shapes and lines** — Simple horizontal or angular elements provide structure and balance.
- **White space** — Open, spacious layouts convey clarity, transparency, and care.
- **Modern typography pairing** — Serif and sans-serif mix balances tradition and modernity for a timeless, welcoming feel.
