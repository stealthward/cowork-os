## Who Brett is

**Core values**
1. Protect your environment: eliminate toxic and high-maintenance people without exception, personally and professionally.
2. Total ownership: outcomes are always Brett's responsibility, never someone else's fault.
3. Pursue hard things: adversity is a signal that something is worth doing, not a deterrent.

**Personal philosophy.** Success comes from relentless pursuit of personal education and continuous improvement across professional, physical, relational, and mental life. Balance comes through equal pursuit, not pulling back. The one controllable variable is effort, so effort and craft improvement are the primary inputs.

**What he wants people to say about him:** "He cares about me and my success as much as he cares about his own."

## Business context

**The model.** Two integrated businesses in San Diego running a flywheel: agents commit escrow business to Five Star, RTM produces content and media that builds their brand at no cost, the stronger brand drives more transactions, and the transactions come back to Five Star as escrow volume. RTM content is the incentive, not the product. Retainers exist only when a client wants more content than their escrow volume supports. No one-off paid projects.

**Compensation structure.** Brett draws no W-2 from RTM and does not plan to. The entire owner W-2 is paid by Five Star. RTM runs to break even or a small profit each year by design; it exists to support Five Star, not to generate income for Brett.

**Revenue goal.** $2.5M to $10-12M. The path is 70-80 escrows a month to 200-250. Average fee $4,000-$5,000, rising with transaction size. Target clients do $50M+ in list-side annual sales.

**Current constraint.** Content quality and bandwidth. Brett needs more personal reps to build authority. The team needs AI tools to produce content at scale, and the offshore team needs AI upskilling.

**Staffing (revised 2026-09-23).** The content distribution person and the additional video editor are no longer open needs. One local videographer alongside Mark remains a future hire, not near term: infrastructure, content, and business development come first, and the role opens once additional accounts are acquired.
