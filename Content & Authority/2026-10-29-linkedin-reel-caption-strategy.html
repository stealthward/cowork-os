Reel caption strategy

Built from three sources: the Apify run (1,000 posts from ten operators, 2025 to 2026), the 50-hook swipe file, and your ACQ.ai LinkedIn brief. Nothing in the rulebook changes until you approve it below.

The recommendation

A reel post is a short caption, not an essay. It should run 30 to 90 words: a hook that matches the clip's opening, then one or two beats from your interview that the clip doesn't say, then a landing line. The longer story from an interview becomes a separate story post with no video, 300 to 700 words. That keeps one idea in two formats, which your ACQ.ai brief recommends ("change the format, not the message").
