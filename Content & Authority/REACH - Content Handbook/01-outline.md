# REACH: Outline / Structure

R.E.A.C.H. = Real Estate Agent Content Handbook. Structure approved by Brett 2026-10-26 (2026-10-26-reach-book-map.md). Beats are pulled from the newest chapter tabs of the R.E.A.C.H. Draft Google Doc; the Doc's own Outline tab is outdated. Update as chapters firm up.

## Working thesis

Most transactions come from someone who already knows you. So the goal of all content is simple: be the first agent someone thinks of when they, or someone they know, is ready to buy or sell. Build an audience of those people and stay top of mind.

## Chapter structure

### Part I. Start Here
1. **Introduction**: thesis; the flywheel vs. the hamster wheel; how Brett came to help agents; what's in it for the reader; who it's for; the five-part outline. Idea Dump seeds: simple vs. easy, good marketing trumps everything, keg-party lesson on supply, demand, and attention.
2. **The Problem This Handbook Solves**: a visibility problem, not a talent problem; how the handbook fixes it; clarity as the payoff; "real estate is simple"; the "you forgot you sell real estate" hook.

### Part II. Get Known
3. **Develop Your Brand**: what brand is and isn't; good, bad, or no brand; why build one; brand vs. branding; define your ideal client; identify their interests; associate yourself with those interests; grow through adjacent audiences.
4. **Build Your Audience**: you can't create demand, only capture it; why agents fail (all real estate, all the time); build an audience that converts; make content your ideal clients care about.
5. **Work Your Three Spheres**: segment by relationship; Sphere of Influence (protect it, content that protects, turn a listing into a content run, the compounding effect); Sphere of Recognition (the growth zone, move people toward influence); Sphere of Proximity (the expansion zone, relationship first, collaboration content, the long game); the binary strategy, Protect or Expand.

### Part III. Get Clients
6. **Attract More Clients**: the Realtor Client Journey (Awareness, Consideration, Decision) with content by stage and sphere; be the trusted guide; lead, prospect, client; Retention and Referrals as the bridge. Draft v4.2 in drafts/ (parked).
7. **Retention & Referrals**: close the gap between willing to refer and actually referring (stat unsourced, verify); content as the bridge; retention, referral, and visibility pillars; the referral flywheel.

### Part IV. Get It Done
8. **Make a Content Plan**: form factors (direct to camera, interviews, voiceover, listing posts); long form vs. short form; content to build your audience (interviews, podcast, personal, community); content to attract clients (personal brand, buyer and seller guides, FAQs, promotional, property tours, property milestones).
9. **Create Good Content**: be the guide, not the hero; capture attention (first 3 seconds, hooks, thumbnails, headlines); use frameworks (Hook, Retain, Reward; 5P; AIDA; PASA; StoryBrand); draw from experience; make it relevant to the platform and segment.
10. **Get Your Content Seen**: distribution (email starting with your SOI, then social); "Quality or Quantity? Yes." (quantity leads to quality; only your audience's opinion counts; bad before good); impressions vs. engagement.

### Part V. Get Started
11. **Get Started**: a one-page plan and the first 30 days; it's intimidating, so just start; embrace the suck; opinions (expensive lifestyle videos don't sell houses); one-page recap of the book.
