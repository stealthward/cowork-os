**Status: Outline only.** Source: working draft.

## Strategy Box (draft, confirm)

- **Angle:** Give the agent a simple plan for what to make: the formats, and the two jobs content does (build audience, attract clients).
- **Frameworks:** Build vs Attract split (ties back to the Three Spheres and the client journey).
- **Takeaway:** "I know which formats to use and which content builds my audience versus attracts clients."

---

## Draft (outline form)

### Form factors

- Direct to camera
- Interviews (host or guest)
- Voiceover
- Listing posts

### Long form vs short form

- "Teenage daughters: scroll AND binge watch" (people do both).
- Quality is the only variable.

### Content to build your audience

1. Interviews
2. Podcast
3. Personal (fun and filler, hobbies)
4. Community (business spotlights; community interest: events, causes, influencer spotlights)

### Content to attract more clients

1. Personal brand
2. Buyer and seller guides
3. FAQs / educational
4. Promotional
5. Property tours
6. Property posts (milestones)
