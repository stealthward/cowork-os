## Draft (raw notes, needs rewrite in Brett's voice)

**Retain:** Maintain a relationship by continuing to communicate and provide value so they use your service in the future and refer others.

### Why it matters

- 88 to 89% of consumers say they'd use or refer their agent again.
- Fewer than 20% actually do.
- The gap between willingness and action is where the opportunity lies.

### Why content is the bridge

- Clients forget names faster than they forget bad paint colors. A well-timed piece of content keeps you top of mind so "I'd refer them" turns into "I did refer them."
- Social content, email newsletters, and short-form videos are gentle reminders that keep you relevant without asking directly every time.
- Consistency builds familiarity, familiarity builds trust, trust drives referrals.

### Framing the opportunity

- Agents who only rely on direct asks capture fewer than 20% of possible referrals.
- Agents who run retention plus referral content campaigns stay visible and multiply that return.

### Referral content strategy framework

**The gap you're closing:** ~89% willing, <20% act, leaving a 70%+ gap. Content turns willingness into action.

**Core content goals:** Retention (keep past clients thinking of you first), Referrals (make it easy and natural to talk about you), Visibility (stay in front of their sphere without being pushy).

**Content pillars:**
- *Retention (direct to past clients):* monthly email with tips or a short video; personal check-in videos (birthday, home-purchase anniversary, holiday); client spotlight features.
- *Referral (subtle CTA):* "I'm never too busy for your referrals" baked into sign-offs; stories of how clients helped friends/family by connecting them to you; social proof posts.
- *Visibility (shareable, community-driven):* local spotlights; market quick-takes; behind-the-scenes "agent life" clips.

**Frequency & format:** weekly social content (short-form video + stills); monthly email/video newsletter; quarterly client appreciation event invite.

**The flywheel effect:** every piece of content is a retention touch; every touch keeps you top of mind; every referral story multiplies into new business.
