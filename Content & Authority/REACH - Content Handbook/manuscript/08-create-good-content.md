# Chapter 8: Create Good Content

**Status: Outline only.** Source: working draft. (Original GPT TOC titled this "Create Good Content"; the working draft also used "Create Great Content." Pick one title and use it consistently.)

## Strategy Box (draft, confirm)

- **Angle:** Good content is interesting and relevant. Capture attention fast, use proven frameworks, and draw from real experience.
- **Frameworks:** Hook / Retain / Reward, 5P, AIDA, PASA, StoryBrand (be the guide, not the hero).
- **Takeaway:** "I have a repeatable method to make content that earns attention and holds it."

---

## Draft (outline form)

1. **Be the guide, not the hero.**
2. **Capture attention.**
   - First 3 seconds
   - Hooks
   - Thumbnails
   - Headlines: recency, relevancy, proximity, conflict. The more you can include, the better.
3. **Use frameworks.**
   - Hook, Retain, Reward
   - 5P
   - AIDA
   - PASA
   - StoryBrand
4. **Draw from experience.**
   - Long ago
   - Recently
   - Current (trending)
   - Other people's experience
5. **Make it relevant to your audience.**
   - Platform
   - Segment
