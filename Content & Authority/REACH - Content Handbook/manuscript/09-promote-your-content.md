## Draft (outline form)

### Distribution

- **Email:** start with SOI (drive traffic to socials, get feedback). Make it easy: headlines, short copy, link to social and website. Content: community.
- **Social.**

### Quality or quantity? Yes.

1. The debate.
2. Tease methods to create quality at scale (covered in Create Good Content).
3. **Quantity:**
   - Quality is the 1st variable of success.
   - Quantity leads to quality: producing in high volume gives you more chances to discover what resonates. Quantity is the path to quality, not its enemy.
   - Repetition is the mother of skill.
   - Volume negates luck.
4. **Quality:**
   - Quality is the 2nd variable of success: once you have attention, creative quality drives success.
   - There's only one opinion that matters, and it's not yours. Don't wait for "perfect." Prioritize speed and volume to create quick feedback loops. Learn what appeals to your audience and do more of it.
   - You will be bad before you're not ("embrace the suck").
5. **Impressions vs engagement:**
   - "It's not about the first impression, it's about the number of impressions."
   - Why they matter (Devore email).
   - Reminder you're in real estate.
   - Credibility when you are referred.
   - Low engagement.
