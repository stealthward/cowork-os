# Rebuild

## Project Identity

This project covers the launch build-out of Rebuild, the show Brett hosts solo (initiative 7a in 00_Resources/initiatives.md). Rebuild opens doors for the partner campaign and overlaps REInvest for investors. Guests come from four categories: investment firm owner-operators; high-producing agents, team leaders, and brokers; adjacent vendors; potential referral partners. This project lives inside the Content & Authority workstation and ends when Brett is hosting (target 2027-03-01) and the show's format, production rhythm, and guest pipeline run from the workstation. When archived, everything learned carries forward into Content & Authority.

Asana project: [REbuild Podcast Build Out](https://app.asana.com/1/459051476492004/project/1214006405857881)

## Workflow

1. Check the Asana project and this folder's MEMORY.md before any session.
2. Guest research: the research-sidekick writes the brief; save it here as `YYYY-MM-DD-<name>-brief.md` (the sidekick's standard format, never .docx, for uniformity) and log the candidate in MEMORY.md.
3. Episode work (guest brief, talking points, content package) follows the Rebuild workflow in Content & Authority/CLAUDE.md.
4. Log decisions (format, frequency, assets, templates) in MEMORY.md the same session they are made.

## Project Rules

- The Rebuild seam: Business Development owns the guest relationship (target selection, pre-show and post-show outreach, escrow conversion); this project owns the episode.
- Brett is the guide, not the hero. Questions frame him as a peer business builder, never a top-producing agent.
- Outreach to guests gives value first and does not pitch Five Star or Real Talk.
- Before proposing a guest, check Zoho and Asana for prior invites, appearances, or declines and state what was found.
